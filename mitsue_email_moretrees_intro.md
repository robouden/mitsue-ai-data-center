# Introduction Email — 一般社団法人 more trees

**Purpose:** First-touch introduction to more trees (代表理事: 隈研吾, 事務局長: 水谷伸吉), inviting an informal exchange about their forest-conservation model and possible fit with the Mitsue reforestation programme.

---

## How to approach more trees

more trees is a national forest-conservation NGO (founded 2007 by Ryuichi Sakamoto; Kengo Kuma became 代表理事 in June 2023) running thinning projects at 11 domestic sites and reforestation at 2 sites abroad. Shinkichi Mizutani (水谷伸吉) has run day-to-day operations as 事務局長 since founding — he is the practical point of contact, not Kuma.

**How we found you:** While researching Kengo Kuma's forest/tree-related work for the Mitsue project's advisory bench, more trees surfaced as the clearest institutional fit — a native-species reforestation and thinning-based forest NGO model that closely parallels what Mitsue is attempting at village scale.

Three rules for the first email:

1. **Japanese first.** A domestic NGO, approached about a Japanese rural forestry project — Japanese-primary is expected.
2. **No partnership or funding ask yet.** First contact requests only a short introductory exchange about their reforestation/thinning model — not site-partner status, not funding, not a Kuma meeting.
3. **Address Mizutani, not Kuma.** He is the operational lead; going straight to Kuma (代表理事, a busy architect) is a common first-touch mistake. Reference his role explicitly to show the email is informed.

### How to find the right contact

- Use the **contact form at https://www.more-trees.org/** (no public direct staff email found)
- Address the message to 水谷伸吉様 (事務局長) by name in the body even though it's submitted through the general form — this signals it isn't generic mass outreach
- General phone if needed: 03-5770-3969

### What to expect

- NGOs of this size typically reply in **1–3 weeks**; do not follow up before two weeks
- A positive reply will likely be a short acknowledgement, possibly forwarding to a program staff member rather than Mizutani directly
- Do not attach full project documentation on first contact — offer the English/Japanese README or introduction A4 only if asked

### After the first contact

If they engage, consider — *in this order*:
1. A short call or written exchange about their thinning/reforestation site model and partner criteria
2. Whether Mitsue's reforestation area could align with a future more trees site (森) or receive advisory input on species selection
3. Any introduction to Kuma or the wider network, only once mutual interest is established

- 御杖村が環境省補助で策定した公式の再エネ導入最大化計画（2025年）の実行を担う位置づけ

My name is Rob Oudendijk, a Dutch national currently preparing a rural revitalization project in Mitsue Village (御杖村), Nara Prefecture, centered on forest restoration and forestry revitalization alongside a small-scale AI data center.

- A 25-year forest restoration and forestry revitalization programme in Mitsue Village, Nara (implemented by the Mitsue Forestry Cooperative, 御杖村森林組合)

- A biomass CHP (combined heat and power) system fuelled by forest thinnings, selling power under Japan's FIT scheme (feed-in tariff) — revenue that funds cooperative workforce growth and equipment, which in turn expands both forestry and reforestation capacity (planned as 2 × 0.6 MWe gasifier gensets)

- Aligned with Mitsue Village's official MoE-funded Renewable Energy Plan (2025), with the project positioned as its implementation vehicle

If you would be open to it, I would greatly appreciate learning briefly about how more trees selects and manages its forest sites and partnerships. This is a first, learning-oriented inquiry — I am not asking for anything specific at this stage.

I am grateful for any consideration you can give to this request.
