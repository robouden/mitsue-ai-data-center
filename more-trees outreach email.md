## English

Dear more trees team,

I'm writing from the Mitsue Village Project (御杖村, Nara Prefecture), where we are building a community-led plan to restore over-planted sugi/hinoki forest to diverse native broadleaf woodland, and to sustain the local forestry cooperative (御杖村森林組合) as the sole operator that plants and tends it.

We deeply admire more trees' region-based stewardship model and your work against monoculture. Two areas where we hope to learn from you:

1. **Knowledge exchange / mentorship** for our forestry crew — mixed-species replanting, thinning-for-value, and connecting our team with one of your existing stewardship sites.
2. **Forest education** — we run a school tree-survey program and eco-tourism activities and would value your experience with "Play with Wood" and community programs.

Longer term, we would be honoured to explore whether Mitsue could become a more trees stewardship site.

Could we arrange a short call to introduce the project? Thank you for your time.

Warm regards,
Rob Oudendijk — Mitsue Village Project

## about more trees (background)

- Forest NPO founded 2007 by Ryuichi Sakamoto; led by architect Kengo Kuma.
- Region-based forest stewardship at 24 sites (Japan + overseas); started in Yusuhara, Kochi.
- Tackles sugi/hinoki monoculture; promotes domestic timber, carbon offset, forest education ("Play with Wood").
- Partners with local governments, forestry cooperatives, researchers, corporations, NGOs.
- Contact: info@more-trees.org — no dedicated form for new sites; email to discuss partnership.
