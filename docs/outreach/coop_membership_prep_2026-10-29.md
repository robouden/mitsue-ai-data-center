## 1. What membership means (general rules)
- **正組合員 (regular member):** an individual who owns forest inside the coop's district (some bylaws set a minimum area, e.g. 10 a), or lives there and works in forestry. Votes and elects officers.
- **准組合員 (associate member):** for people who don't qualify as regular members, if the bylaws allow it. **No vote or election rights.**
- **How to join:** a 加入申込書 with address, name and the number of share units (出資口数; in other coops one unit is often ¥100 to ¥1,000), plus proof of forest ownership if requested (登記事項, 固定資産税の課税明細). A yearly levy (賦課金) may also apply. Shares are returned on leaving only as the bylaws allow.
- **Membership does not give you a say in bids or work orders.** It gives you information, a vote at the general meeting (正組合員), and standing as a landowner.
