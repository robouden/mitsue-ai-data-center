# Note to Prof. Dome — request to attend Kyoto Conference (Sept 23–24)

Context: Dome (堂目卓生) is a confirmed participant on the KIP Kyoto Conference list
(A10, "Advisor to the president, University of Osaka") — see
[[project_advisory_dome_shiozaki]]. He is our project advisor and the natural person
to ask for an invitation/introduction into the event.

---

## English (for Rob)

Prof. Dome,

I saw you're taking part in the Kyoto Institute of Philosophy's Kyoto Conference on
September 23–24. I'd love to be there.

The theme of Inochi — what it means to sustain and renew life — is close to the heart
of what we're doing in Mitsue: reforesting neglected sugi with native broadleaf so
the mountains can support life again, and using that work to revive the village
itself. I'd welcome the chance to talk with people there about countryside
revitalization and reforestation seen through that lens — not just as land management,
but as a question of what keeps a place, and its people, alive.

If it's possible, I'd be grateful for an invitation, or an introduction to whoever
handles participation. Happy to work around whatever format works best.

Thank you for considering it.

— Rob
