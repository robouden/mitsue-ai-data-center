# Questions for Mokurai-oshō / Shaku Myōen (自然宗佛國寺) — October Meeting

General understanding questions — not tied to the Mitsue AI-DC project. Goal: understand who they
are, what 自然宗佛國寺/いのちの森 actually is, and what happened historically. See
[[project_mokurai_genmyo_kagoshima_temple]].

## Who they are / the temple itself

- What is 自然宗 (Shizenshū) as a sect — is it a recognized/independent Buddhist lineage, or
  Mokurai's own founding? How does it relate to (or differ from) mainstream Sōtō-shū?
- What is the history of 佛國寺 itself — the Kagoshima 祖院 vs. the current Mie 寺務所? Why/when
  did the operating base move to Ōdai-chō?
- How did Mokurai-oshō and Shaku Myōen come to work together, and what is her role/title exactly
  (薬師庵主)?
- Does the temple have any other members/followers, or is it essentially the two of them?

## いのちの森 / forest-burial business

- How did the idea for いのちの森 (forest natural burial) come about — was it always meant to fund
  land restoration, or did that come later?
- How does the burial process actually work in practice (since it's explicitly not 樹木葬)?
- How many people have used the service since 2016, roughly?
- How do they think about the legal status question (not being a registered cemetery) — is that
  a settled position for them, or something they're still navigating?

## The land itself

- Confirm exact scope/boundaries of the two parcels (伊勢大杉谷 24ha, 大台町茂原 1.5ha) — any
  interest in sharing 地番/registry detail, or a rough map?
- What condition was the land in when purchased (2007/2014) vs. now — how much has actually been
  restored?
- Given how remote/roadless Ōsugidani is, how do they physically access and work the land?

## Past energy/biomass attempt

- What actually happened with the wood-biomass power venture mentioned in the 2006 land purchase
  — was equipment ever installed, did it ever generate power?
- Why did it stop / not continue?
- Any lessons learned that would be useful to know regardless of whether it applies to any current
  project?

## The 1999–2004 Nara mountain-village project

- Confirm: was the "water-source village in Nara Prefecture" mentioned in their own timeline
  specifically Mitsue, or a different village?
- What was the project actually trying to do, day to day?
- What were the specific "institutional, physical, and mindset" walls that ended it?
- How did the essay/document end up with Teramoto-san's mother specifically — was she directly
  involved, or just someone who kept a copy?

## いのちの森 trademark history (J-PlatPat)

- The trademark いのちの森 (登録4310763) was filed 1998-03-31 and registered 1999-09-03, for
  Class 42 墓地の提供 (cemetery services) — meaning the cemetery-service concept was present from
  the original filing, well before the Ōsugi Valley forest-burial operation itself (2016). What
  was the original plan/context behind the trademark in 1998 — was it already tied to a specific
  piece of land or vision at that point?
- The 1998 filing address was **Komono-chō, Mie** (三重郡菰野町) — not Ōdai-chō. What was the
  Komono-chō connection? An earlier base before Ōdai-chō, or something else?
- A transfer-of-registration notice (移転登録済通知書) is on record for 2009-05-22 — what
  ownership or entity change did that reflect?
- Was 宗教法人自然宗佛國寺 already the corporation's legal name in 1998, two years before the
  temple's own history says the 自然宗佛國寺 rename happened in 2000?

  connected to the land-restoration work or separate?
