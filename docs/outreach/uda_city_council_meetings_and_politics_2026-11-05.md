# Uda City council: meetings and current situation

Source: city.uda.lg.jp 議会 pages and the 2015–Jun 2026 minutes in research/uda_city (crawled 2026-10-29/11-05). Sept 2026 session text is only the 審議結果 PDFs; full minutes for it are not yet published.

## Key people
- 市長 金剛 一智, 副市長 鴻池 昭英 (minutes, Jun 2026).
- 政策監 山本 (new post created 2025, filled by an ex-councillor; controversial in the Mar 2025 council).
- 農林商工部長 東 和浩 (2024); may have changed.

## Sept 2026 session (第3回定例会, ended 25 Sept) — what happened
- **辞職勧告決議案 against Mayor 金剛 passed** (発議第7号, 9/25).
- 監査請求 (audit request) resolution passed (発議第6号).
- A special committee reported on the 農業委員会 neutral-member appointment affair (mayor and several department heads were called as witnesses).
- Petition to keep 榛原東小学校 open: **adopted** (請願第1号). The city plans to merge three Haibara elementary schools in Apr 2027.
- 議案第48号 passed: city to **sue to remove a private building from city-owned land and recover the land**, handled by 農林課. The site isn't named in what I have. Unknown if it relates to the coop or Uchimaki; do not assume.

## What this means for us
- The mayor is under pressure. A resignation or election would slow decisions on new projects. Expect caution, and that officials may avoid commitments.
- Departmental staff (商工産業課, 農林課) still work normally. Keep the 11/12 meeting low-key: introductions and questions only.
- School-reuse is a live, sensitive topic (Haibara mergers, 榛原東 petition). Do not present a school site as a given for us.
- The 2025–Jun 2026 minutes never mention データセンター, and 森林組合 is not mentioned either. Biomass appears once (Jun 2025, in passing). The Estonia project in these minutes is about education and entrepreneurship. So the 2024 "city courting data centers" note is weaker support than it sounded; treat the data center idea as new to them.
