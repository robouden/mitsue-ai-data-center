## Background

We asked Komatsu whether Mitsue Village could be considered for a trial of Komatsu Forest's CTL (Cut-To-Length) forestry machinery, as one possible way to ease the forestry labor shortage. A Komatsu HQ contact replied on 7 October 2026.

Nothing is decided yet: no trial, model, purchase or support. Komatsu is still at the stage of gathering information.
