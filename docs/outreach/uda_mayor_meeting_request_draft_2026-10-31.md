## Why the mayor, and what we want to learn (not pitch)
- 2024-03-22 council: the city's Estonia plan (Cleveron training course; aim to attract the Cleveron factory and **data centers**). 2026-03-18: councillors questioned the Estonia programme and one suggested pausing it and shifting to domestic efforts. So the mayor's own view of IT/data-centre attraction is unclear and possibly changing — ask, don't assume.
- Closed-school reuse is undecided (Haibara 3→1 consolidation, 2026-06-23).
- 内牧小 site is leased free to 宇陀市森林組合 as its forestry base; city said it would cooperate with any coop reorganisation (2024-03-22).

## Questions for the meeting
1. What are the mayor's plans for IT / data-centre attraction after the Estonia programme?
2. How does the city see closed school sites (Haibara) being reused?
3. How does the city see forest/biomass use (timber from Uda City coop), and would it support a small biomass-powered pilot?
4. Is there any support or subsidy path the city would suggest?
