## 1. Logset Oy (Finland) — 8H/12H GTE class, closest match to Komatsu 931XC

**To:** info@logset.com
**Subject:** Inquiry — Japan Market Opportunity for Logset CTL Harvesters (Mitsue Village, Nara)

Dear Logset team,

My name is Rob Oudendijk. I'm part of a small initiative working with Mitsue Village (御杖村)
in Nara Prefecture, Japan, on forest management and renewable energy — early planning stage,
not yet a formal contracting entity.

Japan has almost no domestic CTL (cut-to-length) harvester manufacturing, and until now
Nordic wheeled forestry machines were considered a poor fit for its steep terrain. That's
changing: Komatsu began a CTL trial in Japan in May 2025 (931XC harvester / 855 forwarder),
and labor shortages are pushing the industry toward mechanization generally. As far as we've
found, Logset doesn't yet have a distributor or reference site in Japan — which is part of why
we're reaching out directly rather than through a dealer.

Mitsue Village has a large area of aging, under-managed sugi (Japanese cedar) plantation
forest. As documented in the village's own assembly minutes, the primary constraint on forest
management is not land availability but a shortage of forestry workers. Our plan is to
clear-cut the aging sugi monoculture in planned stages, reforest with native broadleaf species
for wildlife habitat, and use the harvested wood to fuel a biomass CHP (combined heat and
power) plant powering an on-site AI data center. Scaling that up requires harvest volumes well
beyond current levels — CTL mechanization is what makes that workable with the workforce we
actually have.

Mitsue itself is small, but it's not a one-off: this same pattern — aging unmanaged
plantation forest, a shrinking workforce, and biomass/AI-data-center demand pulling harvest
volume up — applies to hundreds of similar depopulated forestry villages across Japan. We're
treating Mitsue as the first proof-of-concept site, with the intention of replicating it
elsewhere once it's working. For Logset, that means Mitsue isn't just a single machine sale —
it's a potential reference case for entering the Japanese market more broadly.

Could you let us know if this is of interest, or point us to whoever handles new-market
development? Happy to share more on the village and the project.

Thank you for your time.
