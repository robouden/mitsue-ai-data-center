Dear Komatsu Forest team,

Thank you again for your help regarding the Mitsue forest regeneration project. I would appreciate your guidance on machine choice for steep terrain.

**1. Machine choice (901XC vs PC138 + C93 head)**
1. For thinning and clear-cutting on steep slopes, under what conditions is the 901XC (with winch) vs the PC138 + C93 head the better choice?
2. What slope (% or degrees) can the 901XC work safely on in Japanese conditions, with a winch and without?
3. Does soil type (rocky, clay, wet) change whether wheeled or tracked is preferable?

**2. Winch**
4. Which winches (T-Winch, Synchrowinch, Ritter, etc.) can be fitted to the 901XC / 855 forwarder, and which do you supply and support in Japan?

6. How is anchoring done (ridge road, standing tree, stump, another machine) in Japanese operations, and are there standard safety rules?
7. Is it correct that with a winch the machine should work along the fall line, not across the contour?

**3. Road building**
8. I understand a winch-assisted 901XC greatly reduces the horizontal strip roads an excavator would otherwise cut. Do you have Japanese examples or rules of thumb (road spacing/density)?
9. Can an 855 forwarder with winch extract along the same lines, removing the need for intermediate landings?
10. On what terrain (cliffs, deep ravines) are roads, tower yarders or excavator benching still needed even with a winch?

**4. Purchase and cost**
11. Purchase price of the 901XC + winch and of the PC138 + C93; rental/lease options; Japanese service and parts support?
12. Are there Japanese sites running 901XC / 931XC with winch on steep slopes that we could visit?

**5. Operators**
13. What training or certification do operators need for steep-slope winch work, and does Komatsu offer a training program?
