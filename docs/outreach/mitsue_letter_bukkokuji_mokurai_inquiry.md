```
Dear Sir/Madam,

Please forgive this unsolicited letter.

My name is Rob Oudendijk, and I am involved in a regional
revitalization and forest-restoration project in Mitsue Village,
Nara Prefecture.

I recently had the opportunity to read an essay titled "Forestry
Administration" (林野行政), apparently written in 1997 by Mokurai
Genmyō of your temple, and was deeply moved by its content. According
to the essay, Mokurai Genmyō walked as a pilgrim across the country
from August 1982 to December 1989, and later, from April 29 to
October 27, 1993, walked roughly 5,000 km across Japan together with
fellow travelers. I wonder whether anyone locally in Bōnotsu might
remember this walking pilgrimage, so I am including this detail in
case it helps.

If you have any information about Mokurai Genmyō, or anyone connected
to your temple who might know more, I would be very grateful to hear
from you.

I apologize for the imposition and would greatly appreciate it if
you could contact me at the details below.

Sincerely,

Rob Oudendijk
[email/phone here]
```
