### English (reference)

Dear Komatsu Forest Business Team,

My name is Rob Oudendijk. I'm part of a small initiative working with Mitsue Village (御杖村)
in Nara Prefecture on forest management and renewable energy.

On August 26th, I sent an inquiry to your Corporate Communications Department about your May
2025 trial introduction of Komatsu Forest's CTL equipment (931XC harvester / 855 forwarder),
asking whether Mitsue Village could be considered as an additional trial site — but haven't
received a reply. I suspect that may not have been the right department, so I'm reaching out
directly to the forestry machinery business contact instead.

Mitsue Village has a large area of aging, under-managed sugi (cedar) plantation forest, and —
as documented in the village's own assembly minutes — the primary constraint on forest
management is not land availability but a shortage of forestry workers.

Our plan goes beyond thinning: we intend to **clear-cut** the aging sugi monoculture in planned
stages and reforest the cleared land with **native broadleaf species**, restoring it as wildlife
habitat. The wood from that clear-cutting fuels a wood-biomass CHP (combined heat and power)
plant, powering an AI data center on-site — and to supply that at scale, we'll need to harvest
well beyond current volumes. Under conventional forestry methods, simply finding enough workers
to do that is the real bottleneck. CTL-method mechanization (fewer machines, fewer operators per
unit of timber processed) speaks directly to that problem — and clear-cut harvesting in
particular is exactly the kind of high-throughput, full-tree work CTL harvesters are well
suited to.

Beyond a trial site, we think Mitsue could work well as a **training ground for CTL operators**.
Building a track record of training people into this new way of working — rather than only
running a technical trial — seems like it could open a new market and workforce pipeline for
Komatsu, with real promotional value as a novel, well-documented use case.

We'd welcome the chance to discuss whether Mitsue could be considered as an additional trial or
training site as your program continues.

Could you kindly direct this inquiry to whoever is coordinating the CTL trial program, or let
us know the appropriate contact? Happy to share more background on the village's forest
conditions and our project.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Rob Oudendijk
