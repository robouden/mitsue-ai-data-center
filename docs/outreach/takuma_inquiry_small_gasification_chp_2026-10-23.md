## English (reference only)

Dear Takuma Energy Plant team,

I am Rob Oudendijk of Mitsue-it, preparing a small wood-biomass CHP project with the forest cooperative in Mitsue Village, Nara, using local sugi thinnings. An experienced industry contact told me Takuma was developing smaller gasification systems.

We plan a ~50 kWe container prototype, possibly scaling to 0.5-1 MWe, on domestic sugi/hinoki thinning chips. Could you tell us:

1. Do you currently offer a small (below ~1 MWe) wood-gasification CHP system?
2. If so, the type, output range and delivery record. If development was paused or stopped, that is useful to know too.
3. Is there an operating Takuma biomass plant in Japan that we could visit (gasification or not)?
4. Who is the right contact for small-scale projects?

Thank you for your time.
Rob
