# First Meeting with Mokurai-oshō / Shaku Myōen — Simple Question List

Goal for this first visit: get to know each other, no agenda beyond that. Full background research
is in [[project_mokurai_genmyo_kagoshima_temple]] and [docs/reference/mokurai_biography.md](../reference/mokurai_biography.md);
the detailed follow-up list is [bukkokuji_mokurai_meeting_questions.md](bukkokuji_mokurai_meeting_questions.md) — save those for a later visit.

1. How did you come to found 自然宗佛國寺, and what does いのちの森 mean to you?
2. How did the essay end up with Teramoto-san's mother in Mitsue — did you know her, or the village, personally?
3. You mentioned Mitsue once worked with the national government and prefecture on new energy — what happened there?
4. What are you working on now?
5. Would you be open to staying in touch after today?

Interpreter needed — Mokurai speaks Mie dialect only; bring one comfortable with that, per [[project_bukkokuji_visit_idea]].
