**English**

Dear Yamauchi-san, Hirose-san and the SANSEI team,

This is still a long way off, but I wanted you to know, because it concerns kiri.

**1. Background.** I asked Forest Energy (Komada-san), the Japanese partner of Volter, a Finnish maker of small wood-gasification CHP units, whether kiri chips can be gasified. They replied on 19 October.

**2. Their answer.**
- Fast-growing species like kiri tend to form clinker (melted, hardened ash) very easily, sometimes before the ash is discharged.
- Countermeasures are essential. If we send a fuel sample, they will analyse it and add chemicals if needed.
- A kiri test is possible:
  - Volume: about 10 t of kiri chips sent to their Akita site
  - Test: about one week of continuous running, with a power-generation report
  - Cost: roughly ¥700,000
- They also sent their chip quality requirements and technical specification.

**3. Please note.**
- Nothing is decided: not the test, and not buying a machine.
- Kiri takes 5-7 years to grow. This is only information on whether kiri could be fuel in the future.
- The clinker concern was already in the BM document Hirose-san prepared, and the maker sees it the same way.

**4. A thought.** If a test happens one day, SANSEI might be able to help, for example by preparing the roughly 10 t of chips. No reply is needed now. I would be glad to hear Yamauchi-san's thoughts when he visits Mitsue. I can also send the documents from the maker if you would like.
