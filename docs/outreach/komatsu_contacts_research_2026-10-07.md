- Komatsu Ltd. runs forestry and agriculture as the **Green Business (Forestry and Agriculture) Promotion Division** (グリーン事業（林業・農業）推進本部), the "third pillar" beside construction and mining. Komatsu Forest AB (Umeå) is a wholly owned subsidiary that builds the machines.
- **Division head:** Executive Officer **梅田博之 (Hiroyuki Umeda)** since April 2025 (IR Day 2024-12-17 presentation on the forestry machinery business; predecessor was 砂田亨, Toru Sunada). One aggregator also lists **Jens Bengtsson** as CEO of Komatsu Forest AB and VP of the Forest & Agriculture Business Division. Both from search summaries, not independently confirmed. Source for Umeda: [Komatsu IR Day 2024 slides](https://www.komatsu.jp/ja/-/media/home/ir/library/results/2024/jp_fy2024_irday2.pdf?rev=9495551eb3484e68acb2002c8f6368af&hash=D4A96D9AEBF219EA197D153F67E829E3).

First test jointly with **Mitsui Bussan Forest Co., Ltd.** (President Yasunori Jinno) on Mitsui & Co. forests in Hokkaido. Sources: [Forest Machine Magazine](https://forestmachinemagazine.com/komatsu-forests-ctl-equipment/), [Nikkei](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUC098L60Z00C25A5000000/).
