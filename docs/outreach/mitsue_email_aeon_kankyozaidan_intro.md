# AEON Environmental Foundation (イオン環境財団) — Introductory Outreach

**Purpose:** First-touch introduction to the AEON Environmental Foundation, pitching the
Mitsue biomass-CHP + AI data center model as a new way to fund old-forest restoration —
positioned as an alternative to thinning-only forestry (low profitability, doesn't fund
itself) and tied to reducing pollen allergy (花粉症) at the source by replacing unmanaged
sugi/hinoki monoculture with native broadleaf.

**Why now:** The Foundation runs 助成事業「イオン環境活動助成」(AEON Environmental
Activities Grant) — ¥100M/year total across awarded projects, for non-profit
organizations, explicitly covering **satoyama regeneration including tree planting**.
Round 36's application window (June 1 – July 20, 2026) has already closed; Round 37 opens
around June 2027. **This email is relationship-building, not a grant application** — the
goal is to be a known, warm contact before Round 37 opens, not to ask for money now.

**Sent 2026-09-12** from rob@mitsue.it — Japanese text only (EN reference text below was not included).

  replacing sugi with broadleaf, not a seasonal countermeasure. Third, frame AEON as a

**プロジェクトの背景と課題:**
御杖村には高齢化・管理不足のスギ人工林が広範囲に残されています。村議会の議事録でも指摘されている通り、林業の最大の制約は土地ではなく**人手不足**であり、間伐だけでは採算が合わず、林業従事者の待遇改善にもつながりにくいのが実情です。

**私たちが提案する新しいモデル:**
間伐にとどまらず、老齢化したスギ人工林を計画的に**皆伐**し、その木材を燃料とする**木質バイオマスCHP（熱電併給）**を主電源とする**AIデータセンター**を建設・運営することで、電力・計算資源の販売収益を森林整備の原資とします。これにより、皆伐→バイオマス発電→AIデータセンター収益→再造林、という循環を作り、林業を「補助金頼みの間伐」から「自立採算の森林再生事業」へ転換することを目指しています。

**跡地の再造林については、野生動物の餌資源となる在来広葉樹を中心に植栽する方針**であり、スギ・ヒノキ人工林を広葉樹林へと転換することは、同時に日本社会全体で大きな経済的損失（年間数千億円規模ともいわれる花粉症の医療・経済コスト）を生んでいる**花粉の発生源そのものを恒久的に減らす**ことにもつながると考えております。対症療法ではなく、発生源対策としての再造林です。

このモデルが御杖村で実証できれば、同様に高齢化したスギ人工林と人手不足を抱える日本各地の中山間地域にも展開可能な、再現性のある地域再生モデルになり得ると考えております。貴財団には、こうした新しいタイプの里山再生事業の**先駆的な支援者**として関わっていただけないかと考え、まずはご挨拶を差し上げた次第です。

**Subject:** A new business model for old-forest restoration and AI data centers in Mitsue Village — introduction to the AEON Environmental Foundation

---

Dear AEON Environmental Foundation team,

My name is Rob Oudendijk. I have lived in Japan for 35 years, the last 15 of them in the forest in Mitsue Village (御杖村), Nara Prefecture — in a self-designed, self-built off-grid house. I now work on a small rural-revitalization project here, combining forest restoration with renewable energy.

I saw that your Environmental Activities Grant program supports satoyama regeneration, including tree planting, and thought our project may be of future interest. I understand Round 36's application window has already closed — this email is not an application, but an early introduction ahead of a possible Round 37 submission.

**The problem:** Mitsue has large areas of aging, long-unmanaged sugi (cedar) plantation. As the village assembly's own minutes note, the binding constraint on forestry here is workforce, not land — and thinning alone doesn't generate enough revenue to sustainably support forestry workers.

**Our proposed model:** rather than thinning-only management, we plan to clear-fell the aging sugi at scale and use the harvested wood to fuel a biomass CHP (combined heat and power) plant, which in turn powers an AI data center. Energy and compute revenue funds the forestry work — turning forest restoration from a subsidy-dependent activity into a self-financing one. The cleared land is then replanted with native broadleaf species chosen for wildlife forage, not replanted as sugi monoculture.

This also addresses pollen allergy (花粉症) at its source, rather than symptomatically: converting sugi/hinoki plantation to broadleaf forest permanently reduces the pollen load that costs Japan an estimated billions of dollars annually in healthcare and lost productivity.

If this model works in Mitsue, it should be replicable across many other depopulating, aging-sugi-forest municipalities in Japan — we see AEON as a potential frontrunner in funding this kind of energy-financed forest restoration, distinct from your existing large-scale community tree-planting activities.

Ahead of the next grant cycle (Round 37, expected around June 2027), I would be grateful if you could let us know whether a project like this would be a fit for the grant's scope, and whether a 一般社団法人 (general incorporated association) qualifies as an eligible non-profit organization under your criteria.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration.
