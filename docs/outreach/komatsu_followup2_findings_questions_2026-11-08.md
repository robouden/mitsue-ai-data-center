## English

**Subject:** Re: Thank you for the Teams meeting — steep-terrain questions on the 901XC / 855 with winch (Mitsue Village, Nara)

Dear Yagami-san and the Komatsu team,

We are still looking forward to your reply to our email of 3 November. Also, the address for Mr. Hasselryd was mistyped, so he did not receive it. My apologies; the summary is below.

**Earlier summary**
- Based on the meeting's "about 30,000 m³/yr per 931XC as a viability guide," we estimated ~0.36 MWe if only C+D grade becomes fuel, ~1.2 MWe if B grade is also chipped (25% efficiency, working assumptions).

**Since then — findings from FFPRI (Japan's Forestry Research Institute) publications**
- Thinning extraction uses vehicle-based machines up to 15–20° slope and cable-based systems above that.
- A Kagoshima model forest used a winch-mounted harvester to cut machine count and reached ≥10 m³/person-day (national thinning average ~3).
- The same sources pair winch machines with dense skid roads; they do not confirm "almost no roads needed."

**Our hypothesis (unverified)**
For large clear-cuts on steep ground, winched 901XC/931XC plus winched 855 should beat building roads with an excavator in both cost and time. At ~100 ha/yr we roughly estimate winch payback in 1–4 years. These are our own assumptions and we would like to replace them with your figures.

**Questions (priority order)**
1. Safe working slope for the 901XC/931XC, with and without winch.
2. How much road construction does a winch actually save on steep slopes (Japanese or European examples/rules of thumb)?
3. Which winches (T-Winch, Synchrowinch, Ritter, etc.) fit the 901XC/855, and which are supplied and supported in Japan?
4. Approximate price of a winched 901XC/931XC + 855 (machine + winch), and lease/rental options.
5. Is the 30,000 m³/yr figure standing timber or delivered logs, and what annual output does one 931XC + 855 set achieve?
6. Can we visit a Japanese site running a winched 901XC/931XC on steep ground?
7. Operator training/certification needed for steep winch work, and Komatsu's training programs.

We can send the full 13-question list if useful. We have no purchase or contract decided and no funding committed; we are still in the sizing stage.

Thank you very much.

Rob Oudendijk
YR-Design / Safecast / Mitsue Village Forest Energy Project
