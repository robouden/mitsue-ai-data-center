# Reference: China's Rural Data Center Strategy ("Eastern Data, Western Computing")

Source: [Japan Today / AFP, Aug 2026](https://japantoday.com/category/tech/china-goes-rural-with-data-centres-in-quest-to-power-ai) (paywalled — summarized via [Techxplore mirror](https://techxplore.com/news/2026-08-china-rural-centers-quest-power.html))

## Summary

China is building AI data centers in remote, rural western provinces instead of its populous eastern coast, under a 2021 national policy called **"Eastern Data, Western Computing"** (东数西算). China expects to nearly double total data center capacity within 5 years amid US-China AI competition.

## Key locations
- **Guizhou Province** — Guian New Area hosts Huawei's largest data center (European-style architecture w/ clock tower, arched bridge), plus Apple and Tencent facilities. Dozens of centers built in recent years.
- **Inner Mongolia** — cited as another key renewable-rich siting region.

## Why rural/western sites
- **Climate**: cooler temps cut cooling costs (relevant to our onsen/altitude siting logic)
- **Renewable mandate**: China requires data centers draw **80% renewable power by 2030**
- **Abundant renewables**: Guizhou/Inner Mongolia have large solar/wind buildout
- **Land availability**: cheap, empty space vs. developed east
- **Grid absorption**: data centers soak up curtailed/surplus renewable power that would otherwise be wasted

## Local economic impact — claimed vs. actual
- Claimed benefits: new roads, universities, local jobs ("all the villagers... now find jobs nearby")
- Researchers' caveat: Guizhou had 7.4%/yr average GDP growth over a decade, but **wage growth ranked second-lowest nationally** — per-capita income gains appear limited
- Guizhou carries heavy debt: 30th nationally in debt-to-revenue, 27th in debt-to-GDP (2024)

## Relevance to Mitsue project
- Direct parallel to our thesis: rural/depopulated area + surplus or curtailed renewable/biomass power + AI compute demand = viable siting logic, at national-policy scale in China.
- Cautionary note for our own economic-impact claims to the village: "jobs created" narratives can outpace actual per-capita income gains — worth tempering promises to 御杖村 accordingly, and pairing job claims with wage/retention specifics (echoes [[project_mitsue_kanko_meeting_2026-08-05_outcome]] — retention, not just job creation, is the real ask).
