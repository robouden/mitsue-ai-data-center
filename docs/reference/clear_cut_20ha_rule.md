# Clear-Cut "20 ha" Rule — What the Official Sources Say

<p align="right">Version: v1.0 | Last modified: 2026-11-07</p>

**Status: UNVERIFIED for 御杖村.** Oba-san said the maximum clear-cut is 20 ha. I found no official Mitsue text stating that, and I could not find the village's 森林整備計画 online. The national rule I could confirm is **not** a 20 ha cap. It is a retention-strip rule.

## Confirmed in official text (read directly)

林野庁 市町村事務処理マニュアル (伐採及び伐採後の造林の届出等), check item (ｴ):

> 少なくともおおむね20ヘクタールごとに保残帯が設けられていないなど市町村森林整備計画に定める標準的な方法と異なり、森林の有する公益的機能の発揮に支障を及ぼすおそれのある皆伐が計画されていないこと

- Source: <https://www.rinya.maff.go.jp/j/keikaku/todokede/attach/pdf/batsuzoutodokede_25.pdf>
- Meaning: a clear-cut needs a 保残帯 (retention strip) at least roughly every 20 ha. This is not a ban on clear-cuts over 20 ha.
- Consequence: without the strip, the municipality can issue a 変更命令 (order to change the plan). The same applies to clear-cutting an entire 林班 at once.
- Procedure: the 伐採及び伐採後の造林の届出 is filed 90 to 30 days before felling. Completion reports are due within 30 days of finishing the felling and within 30 days of finishing replanting. In Nara, the authority was transferred to the prefecture from 2023-04-01 in at least Gojo City, so handling may vary by municipality. See <https://www.pref.nara.jp/7336.htm>.

## Reported by search summaries (not verified against the documents)

- 森林経営計画 approval standard: clear-cut ≤20 ha per site, with the felled area not exceeding 20 ha continuously. Source: 総務省 recommendation, <https://www.soumu.go.jp/main_content/000493645.pdf>
- The limit is set in each municipality's 市町村森林整備計画 and can differ. Gunma uses ≤20 ha except in flat-terrain municipalities (<https://www.pref.gunma.jp/uploaded/attachment/18504.pdf>). Fukui's training document says the same (<https://www.pref.fukui.lg.jp/doc/moridukurika/keikaku/keieikeikaku_d/fil/keikakukenshu_R010524.pdf>).
- Related thresholds: development over 1.0 ha needs 林地開発許可. Under Nara's small-scale rule, 0.3 to 1.0 ha needs a 小規模林地開発行為計画調書 (<https://www.pref.nara.jp/4022.htm>). 保安林 clear-cut needs a governor's permit, and the allowable area is set separately.

## Most likely explanations for Oba-san's 20 ha
1. It is the clear-cut limit in the 御杖村森林整備計画 (令和6年3月).
2. It is the 森林経営計画 standard (≤20 ha per site).
3. It is the 保残帯-every-20-ha rule described loosely.
