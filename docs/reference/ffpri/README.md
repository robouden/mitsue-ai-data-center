## 2. Steep-terrain thinning / winch (relevant to Komatsu question)
- `seikasyu21.pdf` (成果21, thinning in stands needing work):
  - Vehicle-based machines for slopes ≤ 15–20°; cable-based above that.
  - Light cable (tower yarder) = short distance, small area, small load.
  - Tower-yarder avg productivity: clear-cut 4.1, strip thinning 3.2, spot thinning 2.0 m³/person-day (strip ≈ 1.6× spot).
  - Winch-equipped grapple (ウインチ付きグラップル) is cited as the steep-slope adaptation of forwarder-type machines, helped by dense skid-road networks.
  - Cutting rule for splitting on slopes: notch angle b > 90 − slope angle a.
- `p38-39.pdf` (2010): Kagoshima model forest used a **winch-mounted harvester** (+chainsaw) and forwarder to cut machine count, mid slopes, small–mid sites. All model forests hit ≥10 m³/person-day; some 15–20 (vs national avg thinning 3).
- `p10-11.pdf` (2012): European tower yarder; carriage 2× faster empty, 1.2× loaded; ~1.5× productivity of existing swing yarder; needs high-grade forestry roads; earlier Japanese introductions failed (immature resource, poor road network).
- `462.pdf` (Bulletin 462, 2022) and `2022nenpo.pdf` (FY2022 annual report: gasifier ash-slagging, small-scale biomass tech) saved, not yet summarised.
