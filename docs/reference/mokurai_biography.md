# Mokurai-oshō (黙雷和尚) — Biography Notes

Background research on the founder of 自然宗佛國寺 (Shizenshū Bukkokuji), ahead of Rob's planned
October 2026 visit. See [[project_mokurai_genmyo_kagoshima_temple]] for the outreach history and
[[project_bukkokuji_visit_idea]] for the visit planning. Compiled from the temple's own site
(bukokuji.jimdofree.com, morino-ohaka.jimdofree.com), a Wedge Online investigative article, a CBC
News report, and a note.com profile — all secondary/self-reported sources unless noted otherwise.
Not independently verified by Claude beyond reading the cited pages; treat pre-1991 biography
especially as Mokurai's own account, not confirmed record.

## Names

- **Personal name:** 伊藤黙雷 (Itō Mokurai), per the temple's own bio.
- **Religious/public name:** 黙雷 (Mokurai), also 佛國寺黙雷, 黙雷和尚, 勧進僧黙雷.
- **"黙雷玄明" (Mokurai Genmyō):** the signature on the 1997 林野行政 essay found in Mitsue's own
  100-year document set. The 玄明 (Genmyō) component is *not* corroborated by any other source
  found so far — flag as unconfirmed until asked directly.
- Current age: 85 (as of 2026), implying a birth year of roughly 1940–41. No independently
  verified birth date, birthplace, or family background found.

## Self-conception: 勧進僧 (kanjin-sō)

Mokurai identifies not as a conventional temple priest but as a **勧進僧** — invoking the
historical tradition of monks like 行基 (Gyōki), who traveled Japan soliciting public support for
Buddhist works (e.g. Tōdai-ji). His modern version: walk → encounter people → receive support →
build something for society. He treats walking/begging/fasting as the religious practice itself,
not merely fundraising.

Poem he associates with his own role:

> 人をのみ渡し渡して　おのが身は　岸に上がらぬ　渡し守かな
> "I ferry people across, one after another, yet I myself never reach the shore — like the
> ferryman."

## Institutional philosophy — 自然宗

- Presents itself as a return to Shakyamuni's original teaching, deliberately non-sectarian
  (rejects 宗派意識, sectarian consciousness, which he felt Buddhism had become too attached to
  during his pilgrimage years).
- Followers are called **学徒 (gakushō, "students")**, not the traditional 信者/信徒 — the idea
  being everyone remains a lifelong student on the path toward becoming Buddha.
- Explicitly prohibits hereditary succession of 住持職 and 副住持職 (abbot roles) — a temple
  should be a public religious institution, not family property.
- Recognized as a religious corporation by MEXT.

## いのちの森 (Forest of Life) — the forest-burial business

- Run by Shaku Myōen (釈妙円) as 薬師庵主.
- Model: natural-burial fees fund forest restoration/maintenance in a closed loop — payment
  (~¥210,000/person for perpetual memorial care) → forest upkeep → forest becomes the resting
  place → forest survives.
- Explicitly distinguished by Myōen from 樹木葬 ("tree burial"), the more common commercial term.
- Land: figures vary across sources — 243,000㎡ (temple's own site), 255,000㎡ (two-parcel sum
  from morino-ohaka.jimdofree.com: 24ha Ise Ōsugidani + 1.5ha Ōdai-chō Shigehara), ~21ha (Wedge).
  Not reconciled — worth asking directly.
- Legal status is contested: remains are left uncovered by soil rather than technically "buried";
  local objections reported; Mokurai's own stated position is that the site isn't a legal
  cemetery, which he treats as compatible with current law given remains aren't buried. A real
  reputational/legal consideration before any closer or public association with the temple —
  doesn't affect the personal/authorship relationship with Rob, but should inform how visibly
  Rob links the Mitsue project to Bukkokuji in outreach or media.
- The temple explicitly refuses government subsidies on principle: their own Q&A states
  accepting subsidies "robs people of the spirit of independence" and locks recipients into
  "national-prefectural-municipal-forest cooperative control." A real philosophical friction
  point given Rob's project leans on METI/MoE grants — worth navigating carefully.

## Land — physical detail

Two parcels, both within Ōdai-chō but not co-located with the administrative office:

- **伊勢大杉谷 (Ise Ōsugidani):** 24ha, registered 2007-07-05. 12ha 50–60yr sugi/hinoki
  plantation + 12ha natural secondary broadleaf. Extremely remote/roadless mountain terrain —
  satellite imagery inconclusive without land-registry parcel (地番) data.
- **大台町茂原 (Ōdai-chō Shigehara):** 1.5ha, acquired 2014-06-10, held as 基本財産林 (endowment
  forest).
- **寺務所 (administrative office), 〒519-2632 大台町桧原175番地:** just the office — not the
  forest land itself, which is why satellite imagery there shows no forest structures.
- **祖院 (ancestral temple), 〒898-0101 鹿児島県南さつま市坊津町坊7135番地:** still separately
  maintained; forwards mail to Mie. Not a "relocation" so much as two co-existing sites.
