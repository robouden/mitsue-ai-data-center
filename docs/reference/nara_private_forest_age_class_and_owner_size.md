### Derived
- Artificial (plantation) forest = 88.8% of the total. Natural forest = 9.8%.
- 11以上 (51+ yrs) = 6,012 ha, which is **~86–88% of the total**. This is a huge mature, harvest-ready stock.
- Age classes 1–5 (0–25 yrs) total only ~13.6 ha. **Replanting has almost stopped**, so there is a cliff in the age structure.
- Sugi: 4,549 ha, with 4,017 ha (88%) in class 11+. Hinoki: 1,664 ha, with 1,339 ha (80%) in class 11+.

### Data-quality flags (the source's own arithmetic)
- The 民有林計 age classes sum to **6,918.07 ha**, not 7,010.07. The gap is **92 ha**. Artificial (6,227.96) + natural (690.11) = 6,918.07 also, so the stated 総数 7010.07 is probably a typo or includes something unlisted. Ask Oba-san.
- Sugi age classes sum to 4,551.92, versus the stated total of 4,548.92 (3 ha off).
- All other rows sum correctly (人工林計, ヒノキ, 天然林計).

### Derived
- 82% of owners hold ≤5 ha. Only 31 owners (2%) hold ≥30 ha, and 17 hold ≥50 ha.
- 6,012 ha of mature forest spread across 1,485 owners (avg ~4.7 ha total per owner) means **fragmented ownership**. Fuel aggregation for CHP will need to go through a few larger owners, the coop, or consolidation. This links to the 森林経営計画 premium (see memory: Forest Plan FIT Premium).
- The 17 owners with ≥50 ha and the 31 with ≥30 ha are the obvious first targets for fuel-supply outreach.

- Replanting gap: supports the more trees / native-broadleaf reforestation narrative.
- Owner outreach: use the size distribution for targeting.
