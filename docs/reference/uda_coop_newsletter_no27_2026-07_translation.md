## p.10 Notices to members
- Keep member/forest registers updated (ownership changes, inheritance, area changes). Levy is based on official registered area (山林・原野); **forest not enrolled is treated as non-member use**.
- Subsidies need a prior plan and government approval; subsidy procedure reviewed since FY2021.
- **Felling notification changes (from FY2025):** under the specific-thinning promotion plan a 適合通知書 (conformity notice) can be issued via application instead of 伐採届; needed even to sell the timber; notify **≥2 months before felling**. **Clear-cut requires replanting** (exceptions under forest management plans).
- **Forest Management Plan (森林経営計画):** replaces old plan; requires ≥30 ha etc.; subsidy/tax benefits.
- Land-acquisition notification (since Apr 2012) required after purchase/inheritance/gift, even for small areas.
- **Inheritance registration mandatory since 1 Apr 2024:** within 3 years (fine ≤¥100,000); address/name changes within 2 years (fine ≤¥50,000); applies to past inheritances; contact Nara Legal Affairs Bureau.

## p.11 Subsidy overview (as of June 2025)
- Needs forest management plan or specific-thinning plan; thinning plans due **end of August** of application year; before/during/after photos from same spot.
- Forest Resource Management 1st class: ≥100 ha consolidated, 15 ha/750 m extraction thinning/yr; 2nd class: from 0.1 ha. Planting: ≥2,000 sugi/hinoki or 1,000 broadleaf per ha. Under-planting 1,000 / 500. Weeding ages 1–5. Thinning: cut ≥20% (volume ≤35%), **≥10 m³/ha extracted with sales slip**, ages 11–80 sugi / 90 hinoki. Work roads with planting/extraction.
- 美しい森林づくり交付金 (non-qualifying work). **施業放置林整備事業**: city–owner agreement, strong thinning, sugi/hinoki ≤60 yrs.
- Thinning seminars planned (date TBD; sign up with the coop).
- **"Anyone shipping woody-biomass power chip wood via the coop must contact the coop."**
- Services: old/miscellaneous tree felling, hazard-tree felling, forest work/management, work roads; firewood sales.
