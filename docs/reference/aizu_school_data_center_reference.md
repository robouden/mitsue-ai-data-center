# Aizu old-school data center (2011-2012): reference note

Purpose: a citable precedent for pitches (e.g. Uda City / 宇陀市森林組合 on the former 内牧小学校 site): Rob helped turn an empty school into a data center in Aizu as a post-disaster community project.

## What is documented
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Around **September 2011**, Rob (Oudendijk) and Yuka Hayashi were called by a friend to help transform an old school (**Akazawa Elementary School**, Aizu-Misato-machi, Fukushima) into a **data center**, to create work for locals and learning opportunities for the community. | Safecast blog, "Building bGeigies in Aizu", by Rob Oudendijk, published 2012-12-19: https://safecast.org/?p=3183 |
| Rob arrived around Sept 2011 and "the project I was working on in Aizu was finished for me at the 1st of November" (year implied 2011). He helped convert the school into a data center, worked on building bGeigies, and modified the software of one Ninja to act as a fake bGeigie for testing. | same post |
| Safecast then started **building bGeigie radiation loggers in the school's science room**, with local volunteers and staff from "STP". About 30 early units were assembled there. | same post; Rob and the Mitsue Plan reference doc (docs/outreach/sansei_meeting_package_2026-10-22) |
| Safecast later fitted bGeigies to **Japan Post delivery motorbikes** in Fukushima towns. | IEEE Spectrum "Measuring radiation in Fukushima with pocket geigers and bGeigies" (date not confirmed here); same reference doc |
| The Aizu friend/colleague is the ~15-year friend who later ran a biomass plant in Mie (Henry Takata, per people register). | Rob, 2026-09-05 note to Kinjo |
