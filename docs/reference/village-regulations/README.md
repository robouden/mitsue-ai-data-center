## Key findings for the project

**Biomass fuel**
- 木質バイオマスエネルギー供給促進事業補助金 (110001204, 2017): ¥3,000/m³ to shippers + ¥6,000/m³ to procurers of thinned wood supplied as **薪ボイラー用原木** (log-wood boiler fuel). Applicant is the **森林組合** only; recipients must be village residents. Written for the onsen log boiler, not chip CHP. A CHP fuel route would need an amendment or a new line, and this is the natural hook for the mayor conversation.
- 県産材生産促進事業 (110001421): ¥4,500/m³ to the 森林組合 for bringing out otherwise-uneconomic village timber.
- 間伐促進事業 (110001203): ¥20,000/ha to the 森林組合 for thinning done under national/prefecture/village-commissioned projects.
- Fund 森林環境整備基金 (110001398, 2019): holds the unspent 森林環境譲与税 plus budgeted sums. This is the village's flexible forestry money.

**Thinning programmes (who can cut, on what terms)**
- 環境保全型森林整備事業補助金 (110002060, **Mar 2025, newest**): for 自伐型林業者 (self-logging individuals/groups, not the 森林組合) on forest **without** a 森林経営計画 and with no subsidy in the past 5 years. Thinning (≥0.1 ha, 20–30% rate): **¥163,000/ha**. Work road 1.5–2.0 m wide: ¥2,000/m; 2.0–2.5 m: ¥2,500/m. 生活保全林 buffer (30 m from forest edge, ≥0.1 ha) and 危険木 felling: 75% of cost, cap ¥1,000,000 per 集落. Clawback if clear-cut or converted within 10 years of the following fiscal year.
- 施業放置林整備事業 (110001653): Sugi/Hinoki, ≥3 齢級, no thinning in the last 10 years. 40% by stem count, strong thinning. Needs a 協定 between the mayor and the owner. No clear-cut or conversion for 10 years afterwards. Delivered through contractors; the 森林組合 acts as the **マネージャー** (110001419, ≥31 days/year) who finds owners and gets consent, including consent on **how felled wood is hauled out and used**.
- 森林環境整備事業 (110001643): roadside tree felling for hazard prevention. Requested by the 区長, village pays, funded from 森林環境譲与税, contracted to registered bidders.
- 美しい森林づくり基盤整備 (110000854): 50% of standard cost for planting, weeding, thinning, pruning, fencing and work roads. Standard prices follow the Nara prefecture schedule.
- 森林整備地域活動支援交付金 (110001750): national-scheme money for the activity of consolidating owners, marking boundaries and finding owners ahead of a 森林経営計画. Unit rates are in the 別表 image/attachment and are not captured in the text.
- 森林組合運営補助金 (110001299): operating subsidy to the coop; the amount is set by the mayor each year.
- 森林植生保全事業 (110001375): deer-culling bounty via the 猟友会御杖支部, ¥10,000 per doe Apr–Jun, ¥7,000 Jul–Mar.

**Forestry workforce / machinery (relevant to the Kinjo daughter idea and 協力隊 crew plans)**
- 協力隊 forestry machinery loan (110001875, Jan 2024): lendable to **自伐型林業部門 ex-協力隊 members who have finished their term**, for up to **5 years after term end**. Fee **¥1,000 per machine-day**. The village pays inspection, insurance and repair (unless borrower negligence); the borrower pays fuel. Borrowing stops if the member leaves the village or stops working there. Listed: Kubota RX-306E backhoe (Sep 2020) and a 2.7 t dump truck, plus others the village deems necessary. Use limited to work roads and hauling for village forest conservation.
- Other 協力隊 supports: activity subsidy up to ¥300,000/member/year (110001143), start-up support (110001283), housing and renovation (110001139, 110001399), 受入事業所 registration (110001656), and 就業資格取得 (licence) support (110000993).

**Siting / permitting for CHP + AI DC**
- 工場設置奨励条例 (110000425, **1964, old**): eligibility is new capital ≥¥3M **or** ≥30 regular staff; refund of **fixed-asset tax for 3 years** from the start of operation. A case outside the thresholds needs council consent (Art. 3(3)). Apply within 1 month of starting operation. Clawback if the plant is idle, repurposed, scaled back, tax unpaid, or fraud. Wording is very dated (it mentions only 工場/事業所); whether a DC counts as an 事業所 needs a ruling by the village.
- 地域開発指導要綱 (110000444): applies to developments **≥500 m²** (houses, factories, leisure). Needs prior consultation and mayor consent, then a **協定書** before work starts. Developer pays for public facilities and road damage, secures its own water, and meets a BOD 20 ppm effluent limit. Also covers noise/air/pollution, the cultural-heritage check with the 教育委員会, and other permits (農振, 保安林, 森林法, 自然公園法).
- 土砂等埋立て規制条例 (110000446/565/566): soil/fill regulation, relevant to any site grading. Not yet read in detail.
- 補助金交付規則 (110000189): general rules behind every subsidy above.
- 財産規則 / 無償貸付等条例 / 議決条例 (110000151/149/147): rules on leasing or disposing of village assets (e.g. the school site). Not yet read in detail.

## Gaps and cautions
- Nothing in the code mentions data centers, CHP, gasification or 電力 specifically. The nearest hooks are the biomass supply subsidy, the factory incentive, and the development guideline.
- The biomass subsidy only supports boiler logs. Chips for gasification-CHP have no support line today.
- 環境保全型 (110002060) covers forest **without** a 森林経営計画, which matters for small private owners such as the Yamauchi case.
- The raw files are plain text from the site HTML; tables are tab-separated and footnote/form images are missing.
- Not read in detail: 110000446/565/566, 110000151/147/149, 110000420, 110000424, 110001422, 110001399, 110001804. Re-check these before citing.
