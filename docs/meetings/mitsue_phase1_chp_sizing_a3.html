Mitsue AI Data Center - Phase-1 CHP Pilot Sizing (50 kWe, Year 1-3)

How much forest to clear-cut for a 50 kWe biomass CHP prototype, with the current crew plus one hire
Working draft · v1.0 · 2026-09-12
Early planning-stage estimate. Grade-split ratio is a non-Mitsue proxy study; crew productivity is a generic benchmark. Confirm with a stand cruise and the co-op before sizing capex.
Phase-1 target: ~12.5 ha/yr clear-cut, ~37 ha over 3 years (conservative case: only C+D grade wood, ~24% of harvest, is CHP fuel). Current 2-person crew plus 1 hire covers only ~28% of the volume this needs - the gap is the point of Phase-1.
Fuel path Sold elsewhere Workforce Mechanized Dashed = open question
