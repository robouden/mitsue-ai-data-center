Left to right, then down: Current (red) is today's model, and it is a failing loop - forest, thinning, processing, timber sale at collapsed stumpage price (sugi: ¥22,707/m3 in 1980 -> ¥4,127/m3 as of March 2024, still falling y/y), revenue below cost, thinning stalls, neglect deepens, repeat. Phase 1 is the prototype that breaks that loop: the same forest splits into a ~50% thinning (forage returns for wildlife in 5-10 years) plus a small clear-cut sample plot near the village, replanted with native broadleaf by more trees. Processed wood splits by grade - a thinning site typically yields only **~22% A-grade** sawlog with an existing market; the other **~78% (B/C/D-grade)** has no other buyer today and feeds the <50kW CHP prototype (power to the AI data center and Kepco/grid, green = revenue independent of log price), while the A-grade goes to direct timber sale - and CHP revenue funds the next thinning cycle instead of stalling. Phase 3 is full production once the prototype proves out: the same structure scales up - thinning cleanup continues from the 50% baseline, clear-cutting expands to the whole planned area with more trees replanting it all, and CHP capacity grows to 1.2 MW, closing the same self-funding loop at scale (the clear-cut sample plot's own mix skews better than this thinning-only split, since it includes mature, well-formed trees). See mitsue_business_case.md section 2 for the full failure-mode argument and source (Matsushita, 2015, DOI 10.5772/61268); grade split and full offtake proposal in mitsue_fuel_offtake_term_sheet.md. Current stumpage price: 林野庁 山元立木価格統計 via 日本林業経済研究所 (shinrin-ringyou.com/data/kakaku_yama.php). Grade split: 岩手県林業技術センター 研究成果速報 No.308 (larch, illustrative only).

Discussion points for the Mitsue Village Forest Association meeting

  • What would a fair fuel-wood offtake contract look like - price basis, volume, term?
  • Does the current crew size (~3-4) support Phase-1 volumes, or does mechanization/hiring need to come first?
  • Species/replanting method - how much say does Mitsue Village Forest Association want vs. NGO/more trees?
  • Chipper/drying equipment - subsidy-funded, but who owns/operates it?
  • What formal relationship (MOU, board seat, co-op membership) would Mitsue Village Forest Association want before committing?
