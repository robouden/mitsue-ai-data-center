# NAIST × more trees × Mitsue Forest — Collaboration Prep Note (28th mtg)

**Goal of this meeting:** gauge whether NAIST (Kubo / CDG) is interested in a
three-way collaboration with the **Mitsue forest team** (御杖村森林組合 / Mitsue Kanko)
and **more trees**, and agree a first small step if so.

## Follow-up channel (separate NAIST event)
I'm attending **The 5th NAIST Green Economy Session** on **Fri 31 Jul 2026** (12:40–13:20,
CDG Commons, Ikoma). Good opening to **meet Kubo in person** afterward and pick up this
collaboration thread face-to-face — confirm interest, and float the autumn site visit.
(Distinct event; don't conflate with this 28th collaboration meeting.)

## The one-line ask
> "Would NAIST be interested in Mitsue as a *living-lab field site* — jointly with
> more trees on reforestation and our forest team on the ground?"

## Why each side gains (say this)
- **NAIST** — a real outdoor research site, student SDGs field trips, sensing/AI data, publishable work.
- **more trees** — science-backed monitoring for reforestation & J-Credit, a new partner forest.
- **Mitsue forest team** — funding, a workforce/talent pipeline, credibility.

Each brings something the other two can't: NAIST = measurement tech, more trees =
reforestation method + credit channel, Mitsue = the land, labour and biomass.

## Lead angle to pitch
**Wildlife-forage reforestation, measured.** Replant native broadleaf so deer/boar
forage in the forest instead of raiding farms → NAIST studies which species/spacing
works (drone + AI) → more trees plants by its 自然配植 method → **KPI = the village's
~¥5.1M/yr wildlife-prevention spend shrinking** + measurable carbon toward J-Credit.
Field trips fall out of it naturally.

Backup framings if that doesn't land: (2) reforestation MRV / carbon monitoring tied
to our tree-survey app; (3) the "digital green" biomass→AI-DC→forest-carbon loop
(Kubo's actual CDG mandate); (4) student field days as a forestry-workforce funnel.

## First small step to propose
- One **site visit / field trip** to Mitsue this autumn, with more trees.
- A **half-page joint concept note** the three of us co-sign — enough to test a grant
  (SDGs / GX地域共創 / MoE) that *requires* academia + NGO + local partner.

## If they're interested — capture
- Named point of contact at CDG; preferred first activity (visit vs concept note).
- Whether NAIST wants a research question defined first, or a site walk first.
- Any existing NAIST forestry/ecology lab they'd loop in.
