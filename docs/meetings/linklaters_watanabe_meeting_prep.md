## 1. One-paragraph project framing (say this first)

A village-scale project in 御杖村 (Mitsue, Nara): reforest neglected sugi plantations with native broadleaf, fuel a small biomass CHP from thinnings, and use the dispatchable power to run an AI/GPU compute node. Non-profit NGO owns the reforestation mission; a separate operating entity handles energy + compute. We are pre-incorporation, **¥0 committed to date** — funding is pipeline only. We are choosing legal form now and want it right before money moves.

> Keep it honest: no contracts or endorsements are signed yet. Frame everything as early-phase.

## 2. Pro-bono engagement — the ask

1. Does Linklaters run a **pro bono / CSR practice in Tokyo** that could take on a Japan social-enterprise + environmental matter like ours?
2. What **qualifies** a matter for pro bono at Linklaters (charitable purpose, means test, conflict check)? What would disqualify us?
3. If not fully pro bono, is there a **reduced-fee / capped-scope** model for the incorporation phase only?
4. Who at the firm would own an engagement like this, and what's the **intake process** — what do you need from us to say yes/no?
5. Any **conflict issues** foreseeable given the energy/data-center angle (hyperscaler or utility clients)?

> For comparison we've mapped Morgan Lewis's ISH pro-bono track (100% participation, dedicated Impact Investing + Tax-Exempt practices). Useful as a benchmark for what "good" looks like — don't lead with it, but have it ready if he asks who else we're talking to.

## 3. Entity / NGO structure — priority questions

**Legal form**
- 一般社団法人 vs NPO法人 vs 公益社団法人 for a mission-driven reforestation NGO — which, and why?
- Is a **two-entity structure** (non-profit NGO for the forest mission + a for-profit/GK/KK operating co. for energy + compute) sound, or over-engineered?
- Best-practice **firewall** between the two so the non-profit's status isn't jeopardised by commercial energy/compute revenue.

**Governance & risk**
- Board composition (1 vs 3 directors) — liability and control trade-offs for a founder-led NGO.
- Conflict-of-interest policy where the same people sit on both entities.
- Founder protections without compromising public-benefit character.

**Path & timing**
- Can we start as 一般社団法人 and convert to 公益/NPO later? Procedure, cost, timing.
- What must be locked at incorporation vs. safely deferred?

---

## 4. Project-specific legal flags to raise (briefly)

- **Land/forest**: reforestation on land we don't own — lease vs agreement with 御杖村森林組合; more trees-style partnership precedents exist (Tenkawa, Miyagawa).
- **Energy**: biomass CHP ≥1000 kW is FIP-only (not FIT); grid 事前相談 pending. Any regulatory counsel needed?
- **Compute/data**: AI data-center offtake contracts, data-handling obligations — does the entity form affect these?
- **Cross-border**: Dutch founder; any foreign-director/investment wrinkles for a Japanese NGO?
