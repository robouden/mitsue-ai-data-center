Mitsue AI Data Center - Forest-Team-Led Model (Alternative)

Discussion draft - Mitsue Village Forest Association as the operational hub for logging, replanting & processing
Very early draft - concept only. Nothing here is decided or agreed. This is a starting point for discussion, not a proposal to sign.
What's different from the NGO-centered version: Mitsue Village Forest Association (grown to 8 crew) sits at the center and leads day-to-day logging, processing, and replanting work. Formal liaison still flows Village Hall → more trees → NGO → Mitsue Village Forest Association, but the NGO delegates operational coordination to Mitsue Village Forest Association rather than directing it closely. NAIST research support stays direct with Mitsue Village Forest Association.
Government NGO / NPO Company Cooperative (Forest Team) Academia Solid = documented  ·  Dashed = tentative / no signed agreement

Discussion points for the Mitsue Village Forest Association meeting

  • Does 8 crew (up from ~3-4) match the pace Mitsue Village Forest Association wants to grow at, and on what timeline?
  • With more trees liaising Village Hall ↔ NGO, and NGO coordinating with Mitsue Village Forest Association, what does the NGO still need to approve day-to-day vs. delegate outright?
  • Who owns the logging/processing schedule day-to-day - Mitsue Village Forest Association alone, or jointly with the Operating Co?
  • Does more trees want to advise species/ecosystem decisions through the NGO, or have any direct touchpoint with Mitsue Village Forest Association as well?
  • What would Mitsue Village Forest Association want in writing (MOU, board seat, co-op membership) to formalize this more central role?
