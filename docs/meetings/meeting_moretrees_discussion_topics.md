# more trees — Discussion Topics & Open Questions

Internal prep list for the ongoing partnership conversation with **more trees**
(Ryuichi Sakamoto / Kengo Kuma NGO). First meeting held **2026-07-14** with
**Kishi Takuya (kishi@more-trees.org)** and **Miyazaki Yu (miyazaki@more-trees.org)**;
strong overlap on **reforestation, education, corporate engagement, and sponsorship**.

**Working model:** more trees takes the *forest / government / replanting* layer; our NGO
takes the *biomass CHP + AI data center* layer. Harvested sugi is the interface — our
energy+compute revenue funds the clearing, more trees replants it; the Mitsue Forest
Cooperative (御杖村森林組合) does the physical work for both. Reforestation values adopted
in `mitsue_forest_workforce_energy_plan.md` §9.

---

## A. Partnership shape
- Formal co-developed partnership, or lighter advisory / knowledge-exchange to start?
- Comfortable with a **two-agreement structure** — more trees × village (forest / regional
  revitalization), our NGO × village (energy / DC), plus a short inter-party MOU?
- Which entity is the **counterparty to the village** for the forest piece — them, us, or joint?
- Any **exclusivity / naming** expectations (e.g. "more trees site")?

## B. Reforestation & method
- Confirm method lead: **local-provenance seedlings + natural planting-arrangement (自然配植)
  + assisted natural regeneration**. Is Miyawaki / Niwamori dense-planting off the table, or
  one option per site?
- Who owns **species selection** — them, our NGO, or joint (wildlife-forage priority)?
- Can they run **local-provenance seed-collection & nursery** in Mitsue, or sourced via
  Odai / Miyagawa?
- View on **harvest-pace vs replant-pace** — can a rotation / mixed sugi regime coexist with
  their deliberate "dialogue with the forest" cadence?
- **Deer / boar mitigation** — reuse Tenkawa's 12 m reinforced-net enclosures?

## C. J-Credit / carbon
- **Attribution:** who owns the credits — more trees, our NGO, or landowners?
- Join their **existing Tenkawa / Odai registration**, or register Mitsue separately?
- Who runs annual **monitoring / reporting**?
- Expected **tCO₂/yr and revenue-split** logic.

## D. Education
- Fold their grade-by-grade **seed → raise → plant** model into our **tree-survey app +
  Mitsue Elementary program** — one joint curriculum?
- **JUBAKO** office seedling kits as a sponsor / engagement hook — relevant here?
- Tours / site visits — co-run with their existing tour channel?

## E. Companies & sponsorship *(largest overlap)*
- Route corporate sponsors through **their Corporate Forest channel**, run our own, or **co-brand**?
- The hook: **"companies sponsoring the forest that powers an AI data center"** — do they want
  to lead, co-sell, or stay forest-only?
- Any **conflict / overlap in target companies** (their roster is large — SMBC, Salesforce…)?
- Revenue / attribution split on sponsorships that touch both halves.

## F. Government & funding
- Will they **sign / co-sign the village agreement**, and does their brand strengthen our
  subsidy applications (METI GX regional co-creation / MoE zero-emission DC)?
- Do any of **their funding lines** (Mirai Fund, Forestry Agency broadleaf, prefecture) apply
  to Mitsue's replant?
- **more trees participation is contingent on village-government go-ahead** — confirm this
  is understood on their side.
- Kishi-san asked **us to verify** the village doesn't already have an existing plan/project
  that would proceed without more trees and without our ideas — check before the next meeting.

## G. Brand & risk *(theirs to protect)*
- What **sustainability assurances** do they need to put their name on a project that also
  *harvests* sugi for fuel?
- Any **red lines** — scale of clearfell, compute / energy optics, data-center association?

## H. Logistics / next step
- **Next meeting or site visit** to Mitsue? Who from their side attends?
- What do **they need from us** first (one-pager, EVM summary, site data, forest plan §9)?
- Point of contact + cadence going forward.
