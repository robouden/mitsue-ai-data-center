# Mitsue Village — Forest Area: Before / After (25-Year Projection)

**Prepared for:** 上坂奈穂様 (Nao Uesaka), Mizuho Securities — Sustainability Promotion Dept.
**Purpose:** Order-of-magnitude estimate of how much sugi/hinoki plantation area the project's biomass-CHP-funded workforce could realistically thin and convert toward native broadleaf over 25 years, and what that implies for energy output.

**Status:** These are modeled projections from the project's Forest Twin calculation, calibrated against a real empirical anchor (Mishima, Fukushima — NIES 2020, ~750 t/yr feeding ≤50 kWe). They are not measured or committed figures.

---

## Before (today)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Total village forest | ~7,051 ha (>90% privately owned, postwar sugi/hinoki plantation) |
| Annual thinning (current co-op capacity) | ~66 ha/yr |
| Wood actually recovered | only ~120–300 m³/yr |
| Forest cooperative crew | small, average age ~33 |

The gap: thinning today is subsidy-driven and leaves the cut wood largely in the forest — it is not a mechanized harvest, so almost none of the cleared area is followed by active broadleaf replanting.

## The mechanism

Biomass-CHP revenue from harvested sugi thinnings pays for a larger, mechanized cooperative crew (harvester + forwarder, chipper, drying). A bigger crew thins more forest faster; the cleared area is where native broadleaf replanting (see companion species list) becomes viable, restoring wildlife forage and reducing deer/boar crop damage. The same harvested wood fuels the CHP plant that powers the AI data center — energy output and forest restoration are the same lever, not two separate programs.

## After — 25-year scenarios by workforce scale

| Cooperative capacity | Harvest rate | **Area converted in 25 yr** | Wood/yr | Biomass plant | Electricity/yr |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current + light mechanisation | ~70 ha/yr | 1,762 ha (25% of forest) | 10,500 m³ | ~290 kWe | ~2,000 MWh |
| **Double crew + harvester/forwarder — recommended baseline** | **~140 ha/yr** | **3,526 ha (50% of forest)** | 42,000 m³ | **~1,150 kWe** | **~8,000 MWh** |
| Triple crew, full mechanisation | ~200 ha/yr | ~5,000 ha (~70%) | ~60,000 m³ | ~1.6 MWe | ~11,000 MWh |
| Optimistic (~4× crew) | 254 ha/yr | 6,346 ha (90%) | 135,000 m³ | 3.7 MWe | ~26,000 MWh |

**Recommended baseline: doubled workforce — roughly half the village's forest (~3,500 ha) thinned and opened to broadleaf restoration over 25 years, supporting a ~1.1–1.2 MWe biomass plant.**

## Real-world precedents used to sanity-check this

| Village | Forest | Harvest model | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shimokawa, Hokkaido (下川町) | 4,500+ ha | ~50 ha/yr, 60-yr sustained cycle | 56% local heat self-sufficiency |
| Nishiawakura, Okayama (西粟倉村) | 93–95% forest | ~3,000 ha under 100-yr management | forestry-jobs revival |
| Mishima, Fukushima (三島町) | ~88% forest | ~750 t/yr | ≤50 kWe — our model's calibration anchor |
