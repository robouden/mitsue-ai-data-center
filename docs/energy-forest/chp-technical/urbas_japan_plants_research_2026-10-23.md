## Aridagawa Biomass Power Plant (details from [SymEnergy](https://www.symenergy.co.jp/service/area_energy/aritagawa/))
- **Operator:** 有田川バイオマス株式会社 (local investors). **Design/construction:** シン・エナジー株式会社 (SymEnergy, HQ Kobe). This corrects the table: Sym Energy built it, the operating company is Aritagawa Biomass.
- **Output:** 900 kWe total = 2 x 450 kWe Urbas units; ~6,700 MWh/yr, ~8,000 h/yr, sold to 関西電力送配電.
- **Fuel:** ~10,000 t/yr unused local wood, all from Wakayama.
- **Heat:** supplied to neighbouring かなや明恵峡温泉; also dries fuel wood/chips; char planned as fertilizer. (Cf. our onsen/heat-distance notes.)
- **Address:** 和歌山県有田郡有田川町 (126 Kanakuki, Shuri-gawa, Aritagawa-cho). Site ~10,000 m².
- **Status:** COMPLETED 2022-10-13 (company release [news_20230131.pdf](https://aridagawa-biomass-co-jp.secure-web.jp/pdf/news_20230131.pdf)); SymEnergy page header 開発中 is stale. Rep. director 原見健也.
- **Community fund:** 2022-10-12 to 12-30, ¥5M target fully raised via エントライ (Plus Social Investment), 46 investors (15 in Wakayama = ¥2.84M), ¥20,000/unit, 3-yr, target 103.81% repayment, used to buy Wakayama wood; perks = onsen tickets and local goods. Model for village-investor idea; ask about it on the visit.
- **Visits:** page says tour requests/inquiries go to Aritagawa Biomass Co. via the inquiry link on the Aritagawa page. No phone/email found in text.
- SymEnergy HQ: 〒651-0087 神戸市中央区御幸通8-1-6 神戸国際会館14F. Contact form: https://www.symenergy.co.jp/contact/ (not read).
- Caution: SymEnergy also contracted with BioEnergie Wegscheid (2023) for Ikuno-Ginzan; Wegscheid/Entrenco is insolvent (2026-04), so check status.

## Technology and fuel
- Fixed-reactor wood gas, filter-cleaned, gas engine; Urbas range 150-450 kWel per its [brochure](https://www.urbas.at/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/p_URBAS_DE_KWK_7.8.web_WF.PDF-jp.pdf); first CHP 2008, commercial 2010.
- Recommended chip spec: moisture 5-15%, 4.2-4.8 kWh/kg. **Implication:** needs drying, relevant to the sugi moisture/drying notes.
- Japanese-language presentations: [2017](https://www.advantageaustria.org/jp/events/2017.05.25_-_Biomass_6_Urbas__sJPN.pdf), [2022](https://www.advantageaustria.org/jp/events/20220914_Urbas.pdf).

## Relevance
- 450 kWel modules are 9x our 50 kWe prototype but match the later 0.5-1 MWe phase and the Phase-4 forest-workforce plan.
- Two operating Japan sites on low-value wood is exactly what we want to see: fuel prep, drying, tar/filter O&M, uptime, heat use.
