Key results (placeholders, base case)

  • 50 kWe, electricity only: EBITDA about -¥8M/yr (break-even about ¥53/kWh before capital)
  • 50 kWe with heat + compute: payback about 5 yr (2/3 subsidy) to 15 yr (none)
  • 1.2 MWe: positive only if fuel ≤ about ¥5,000/t fresh
  • Kiri as fuel: about ¥0.4M revenue per ha per cycle vs about ¥2M to plant
  • CO2: 50 kWe displaces about 180 t/yr, about ¥1M/yr at ¥5,600/t
