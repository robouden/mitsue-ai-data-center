<p align="right">Version: v1.2 | Last modified: 2026-09-26</p>

Scans received by post from 釈妙円 (Mokurai Genmyō), 自然宗佛國寺 (Shizenshū Bukkoku-ji). This batch contains three distinct document sets, bundled/interleaved together in the scanning:

- **A. Natural-burial dispute correspondence** (Ōdai-chō / Mie Prefecture, ongoing 2025–2026) — pages S9090011, S9090013, S9090017, S9090018.
- **B. "Forest Nation / Energy and Living School" biomass project records** (Mie Prefecture → NEDO, 2005) — pages S9090012, S9090014, S9090015, S9090016, S9090019.
- **C. 大杉谷地域再生機構 (Ōsugidani Regional Regeneration Organization) forest-fund/wellness-retreat project** (Ōdai-chō, 2006) — pages S9090020, S9090021, S9090022, S9090023.

Source scans: `docs/energy-forest/source-scans/from temple/S9090011.jpg` through `S9090023.jpg`. (S9090001a.jpg transcribed separately — see [mokurai_letter_2026-09-23_transcription.md](mokurai_letter_2026-09-23_transcription.md).)

**Pattern across sets A–C:** all three involve Mokurai (or the same organizational lineage) attempting a region/forest-revival project in the Ōdai-chō / Ōsugidani area, each meeting resistance from local government, the press, or both — biomass energy (2005), a wellness-retreat/forest fund (2006), and the natural burial ground (2007–present, disputed 2023–2026). The current dispute is the latest round of a two-decade-long pattern, not an isolated conflict.

### A2 — S9090013: Background article/summary on the burial dispute

**Japanese:**

この現場での自然葬が始まったのは2007年にさかのぼる。
「自然宗佛國寺」という宗教法人が約4500坪のこの山林を買い取り『森のお墓・いのちの森』と名付け、同時に本山を現在の大台町に移転した。
自然葬は現在12区画に及び、永代供養の場合は1人21万円となっている。
佛國寺の黙雷和尚がこう話す。「昨年11月に木の骨壺3個が掘り返され、遺骨が散乱しました。その後、町は現場確認もせずに寺に来て一方的に『墓地埋葬法（墓埋法）違反だ。町に墓地の申請をしなさい』と言われました。こうした状況を地元紙が報道し、騒動の発端となったのです」

**English translation:**

Natural burials at this site date back to 2007.
The religious corporation "Shizenshū Bukkoku-ji" purchased this roughly 4,500-*tsubo* (~14,880 m²) tract of forest, named it "Forest Grave / Forest of Life" (森のお墓・いのちの森), and at the same time relocated its head temple to what is now Ōdai-chō.
Natural burials now number 12 plots; perpetual memorial services cost ¥210,000 per person.
Bukkoku-ji's chief priest Mokurai explains: "Last November, three wooden burial urns were dug up and the remains scattered. Afterward, the town came to the temple without even confirming the situation on site, and unilaterally told us, 'This violates the Cemeteries and Burial Act (Bochi Maisō Hō) — you must apply to the town for a cemetery permit.' The local newspaper reported on this situation, which became the spark for the whole dispute."

**Japanese:**

事件の起因は、自然保護活動家を自認する煽動者の十九年有半にわたる悪辣な誹謗中傷と妨害にあるが、問題をこじらせたのは　大台町副町長（県から出向の女性）にある。

・昨年六月二十六日、大台町税務住民課長　来訪。はじめて森林自然葬『いのちの森』の現場を確認して「埋蔵でなかったっことを認知。

・七月二日、大台町当局が電話で「県を通して国と相談する」（国に問う）と通告。

（副町長　面会拒否）

・八月二十日、税務住民課長を介してやっと副町長に電話がつながる、副町長＝「こちらは行政ですから、国から墓地埋葬法違反だとの返事がきたら　あなたはどうされますか。」

**注＝**現場を確認した税務住民課長が、中京テレビ、週刊誌等の取材に「埋蔵ではなかった」と答えているにもかかわらず、副町長は、自分の描いたストリー以外の人の意見は理解できないのか「国が、行政ですから、私は行政ですから」と言葉の繰り返しで、話が噛み合わず　物別れに終る。

**English translation:**

**The Mental Wall of the Hard-to-Communicate-With Deputy Mayor of Ōdai-chō**

The root cause of this affair lies in nineteen and a half years of vicious slander and obstruction by an agitator who calls himself a nature-conservation activist. But what made the problem worse was the Deputy Mayor of Ōdai-chō (a woman seconded from the prefecture).

- Last year, June 26: the head of Ōdai-chō's Tax & Residents' Affairs Division visited. On confirming the site of the forest natural-burial ground "Forest of Life" for the first time, he acknowledged that it "was not [unlawful] interment."
- July 2: Ōdai-chō authorities notified [us] by phone that they would "consult with the national government through the prefecture" (i.e., put the question to the national government).
  (The Deputy Mayor refused to meet in person.)
- August 20: finally reached the Deputy Mayor by phone via the division head. The Deputy Mayor: "We are the administration, so if the national government replies that this violates the Cemeteries and Burial Act, what will you do?"

**Note:** Even though the division head who had inspected the site told Chūkyō TV and weekly magazines in interviews that "it was not [unlawful] interment," the Deputy Mayor seemed unable to accept any view other than the narrative she had constructed, repeating only "The national government is the administration, and I am the administration" — the conversation never meshed, and it ended in a complete breakdown.

### A4 — S9090018: "大台町町民福祉課長の不可解な言動" (The baffling conduct of Ōdai-chō's Citizens' Welfare Division head)

**Japanese (framed box, right column):**

国家権力を笠にかる　県・町役人の理不尽な仕打ちには断じて屈伏しない。虎の威をかる。――生ある限り。

**大台町町民福祉課長　の不可解な言動**

・文部科学大臣認証『宗教法人自然宗佛國寺』の公益事業『いのちの森』の附帯事業＝森林自然葬（森のお墓・いのちの森）が一昨年（令和5年）十一月荒らされ　遺骨が散乱。誰がこんな非道なことをするのかと思っていたところ、大台町町民福祉課長がアポイントもなく突然来訪。佛國寺の聴取、現場確認もせず「墓地埋葬法違反」だと断罪。

黙雷＝木の骨壺三個が荒らされたが、（下の道路からは見えない）（下から見て確認した）課長＝下の道路からは見えない場所を、佛國寺所有山林に無断で入って墓地埋葬法違反だと確認したのか。

・一昨年十二月十三日、町民福祉課長、アポイントもなく突然来訪。高圧的に「墓地埋葬法違反だから県と相談して法的手続をすすめる」と通告。

そして、詳しくは『夕刊三重』（令和5年12月12日）を見よとのこと。

翌日、『夕刊三重』を取り寄せて、記事を一読し、町当局の「先ず、はじめに墓地埋葬法違反ありきの記事」の姿勢がよくわかった。

**注＝**「夕刊三重」の記事にある写真の場所は、木の骨壺が荒らされた場所。（下の道路からは見えない）
しかも佛國寺所有山林に無断で入って撮影。また当方の取材もない捏造記事。

**English translation:**

We will absolutely not submit to the unreasonable treatment of prefectural and town officials who hide behind the authority of the state — an ass borrowing the tiger's might — for as long as we live.

**The Baffling Conduct of Ōdai-chō's Citizens' Welfare Division Head**

The ancillary project of "Forest of Life" (the public-interest undertaking of the religious corporation Shizenshū Bukkoku-ji, certified by the Minister of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology) — the forest natural burial ground ("Forest Grave / Forest of Life") — was disturbed in November of the year before last (Reiwa 5 / 2023), with remains scattered. While we were wondering who could do something so inhumane, the head of Ōdai-chō's Citizens' Welfare Division suddenly showed up without any appointment, and — without interviewing Bukkoku-ji or even confirming the site — condemned it outright as a "violation of the Cemeteries and Burial Act."

Mokurai: Three wooden burial urns had been disturbed (in a spot not visible from the road below — confirmed by looking from below). Did the division head enter Bukkoku-ji's privately owned forest without permission, in a place invisible from the road, in order to "confirm" this so-called violation?

- On December 13 of the year before last, the Citizens' Welfare Division head again showed up suddenly without appointment, and high-handedly announced: "Since this is a violation of the Cemeteries and Burial Act, we will consult with the prefecture and proceed with legal procedures."

We were told to see the *Yūkan Mie* (evening Mie) newspaper of December 12, Reiwa 5 (2023) for details.

The next day, we obtained a copy of *Yūkan Mie* and read the article, which made clear the town authorities' stance of "starting from the premise that it was a violation of the Cemeteries and Burial Act."

**Note:** The location shown in the photo accompanying the *Yūkan Mie* article is the spot where the wooden urns were disturbed (not visible from the road below). Moreover, it was photographed by trespassing without permission onto forest land owned by Bukkoku-ji. It is a fabricated article, written without ever interviewing our side.

Rob flagged the 2005 "森の国・エネルギーと暮らしの学び舎" biomass application (section B) as the most important thread to run down. Web research (2026-09-26) found no independent trace of it beyond the scanned documents themselves. Searches tried, all without a hit on this specific project:

None of these surfaced the project, an adoption/rejection decision, or any technical plan. - Full advisor/feasibility-committee roster existed, with high-level backing (TEPCO, two sitting Diet members, METI Chubu Bureau, Forestry Agency's Kinki-Chugoku Regional Forest Office, NEDO as observer) — see section B4.
