# Tracked vs. Wheeled Harvesters — Road Construction Impact & Options for Mitsue

## The core tradeoff

Wheeled harvesters (Ponsse, Komatsu Forest, Logset, Rottne) need **more** road/trail
preparation, not less: they require graded, compacted surfaces for traction and stability
and are generally limited to ~30% slope. Tracked machines have lower ground pressure and
higher breakout force, so they can work steeper slopes (~40%) on rougher, less-prepared
ground — which is why Japan's steep terrain favours excavator-based (tracked) harvesters
over European wheeled designs.

Sources: [USDA FS chassis configurations](https://www.fs.usda.gov/forestmanagement/equipment-catalog/chassis-config.shtml),
[MDPI steep-terrain harvesting study](https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4907/14/7/1430)

## Options for Mitsue (least road construction first)

1. **Excavator-based tracked harvester** — small (7–13t) short-tail-swing excavator base +
   stroke-feed harvester head (KESLA 16RH/20SH, or Log Max/Waratah equivalents). Japan's
   dominant pattern: the swing-boom base fells/processes/loads without wide graded roads,
   just narrow skid trails. Domestic base machines: Hitachi (ZX75US-3, ZX135USL-7),
   Komatsu, Yanmar. ([hitachicm.com/jp](https://www.hitachicm.com/jp/ja/products/industry/forestry/),
   [Kesla](https://kesla.com/en/products/category/forestry))

2. **Iwafuji (イワフジ工業)** — Japanese domestic maker building harvesters purpose-designed
   for Japan's steep, narrow-road conditions. Worth checking against the project's
   domestic-maker preference (see [[feedback_domestic_biomass_maker]] — same logic for
   forestry machinery as for CHP). ([iwafuji.co.jp](https://www.iwafuji.co.jp/products/forestry_harvester))

3. **Tower-yarder + excavator-processor combo** (Shimane Pref. model) — for slopes too
   steep even for tracked excavators: cable yarding pulls logs to a landing where the
   excavator-based harvester processes them, avoiding any new road into the stand at all.
   ([MDPI steep-terrain study](https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4907/14/7/1430))

## Relation to Komatsu/Logset/Vimek outreach

Komatsu Forest, Logset, and Vimek (see [[project_komatsu_ctl_outreach]] and
`docs/outreach/mitsue_email_logset_vimek_outreach.md`) are wheeled/CTL trial-site
outreach targets — useful for equipment donation/trial partnerships, but their machines
are the higher-road-requirement option. If road construction cost/impact is the binding
constraint, the excavator-based tracked route (option 1/2 above) should be the primary
recommendation, with wheeled CTL kept as a secondary/flatter-terrain option.
