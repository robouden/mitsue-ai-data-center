Open questions

  1. Village-owned forest (村有林)? Asked 2026-08-05, no answer.
  2. Common-use land (財産区・入会)? No Mitsue record; only Tenkawa confirmed.
  3. Independent harvester's wood: his own goes to Misugi market; others reportedly to Sakurai coop. Coop flows unverified.
  4. How many other independent harvesters? He estimates 10+. Village asked 2026-09-30.
  5. Does the coop thin members' forest, or do owners deal with harvesters directly? Ask Oct 2.
  6. Reforestation: 神末 (cherry) and 土屋原 (planned). Who funded and did it?
  7. Who is the 施業放置林マネージャー and is there an owners' list or group?
  8. Onsen wood: which grades and volumes, and from whom besides the coop?
  9. The "6-year" contract term has no source. Only the 3-year 協力隊 term and 5-year machinery loan are documented.
  10. B-grade destination (plywood buyer or CHP fuel) sets real CHP fuel volume.
Grade split (A 22% / B 54% / C-D 24%) is from a larch study, not Mitsue. Not shown: felling notices (伐採届), 森林経営計画, subsidies and the forest environment tax. Onsen heat cannot be piped from 牛峠工場. Internal working draft.
