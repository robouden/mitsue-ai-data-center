# Mitsue Village Forest Association — Workforce & Relocation Strategy

> **"Rewild a mountain, power an AI data center, and rebuild a village —
> one job, one place, one 25-year story."**

Doubling (and eventually tripling) the 御杖村森林組合 (Mitsue Village Forest Association) crew is the single
lever that unlocks the whole forest→energy plan (see
`mitsue_forest_workforce_energy_plan.md`). This document sets out how to attract the
right people — **mission first, money as the enabler** — using Japan's existing
forestry and relocation subsidy stack.

The core thesis: **subsidies get us to parity; the mission is what actually attracts and
retains.** Grants remove every financial reason *not* to come; the mission is the reason
*to* come — and it is the fix for the well-known 3-year retention problem of relocation
schemes.

---

## 1. The pull — a three-pillar mission

The ordinary rural-forestry pitch ("come do sugi thinning in a depopulating village") is
a hard sell. Ours is categorically different: the same job restores a forest, powers
computation, and revives a village. Each pillar widens the funnel to a different
community.

### 🌱 Reforest — restore a living native forest
Not "cut trees" but the opposite: **convert dark, lifeless sugi monoculture into living
native broadleaf forest** — コナラ / クヌギ for wildlife forage, bringing back birds,
insects, and balance; protecting watersheds and reducing landslide risk. It is a
**multi-decade legacy you can physically walk through**: *the forest our ancestors
planted for timber, remade into the forest we leave our descendants.* This is the
emotional core, and it pulls **ecology- and conservation-minded people** who would never
answer a "forestry worker" ad but would move to *rewild a mountain*.

### ⚡ Power — energy + a community AI data center
The same forest work **literally powers computation** — biomass CHP, salt-battery
storage, a village-owned data center. Frontier technology, not analog forestry. Pulls
**purpose-driven technologists and engineers**.

### 🏡 Revive — rebuild a village with a future
A depopulating mountain village given a genuine next chapter — jobs, families, energy
self-sufficiency, disaster resilience. Pulls **U/I-turn returnees** who want rural life
*with* a future, not nostalgia.

**Why the triad matters:** it is the project's whole thesis in one job —
*forest restoration → distributed energy → community-owned compute*. Most rural jobs
offer one thread; this offers all three, woven together. That breadth is both the
attraction and the retention glue.

## 2. Who we are recruiting (personas)

| Persona | Drawn by | Reach through |
|---|---|---|
| **Conservationists / ecologists (20s–40s)** | Reforest pillar — rewilding, biodiversity | Conservation NGOs, rewilding communities, ecology programmes |
| **Purpose-driven technologists / engineers (20s–30s)** | Power pillar — energy + data center; city-burnout | Climate-tech communities, the project's network (Ray Ozzie / Joi Ito reach), media |
| **U/I-turn returnees & young families** | Revive pillar — rural life with a future | 移住 fairs, JOIN, prefectural relocation portals |
| **Forestry + CS/engineering students** | The rare crossover of all three | University forestry & CS departments, internships |

These people do **not** read job boards for "森林組合作業員." The formal listing is
necessary (for subsidy eligibility) but the real reach is the mission story through
communities and media.

## 3. The stack — enabling subsidies (2026, verified)

| Scheme | Funder → recipient | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **緑の雇用 (Green Employment)** | 林野庁 → **the co-op** | Wage-equivalent + OJT training subsidy for new forestry recruits (~3-yr フォレストワーカー) | **Core lever** — removes the co-op's cost/risk of taking on green workers |
| **地域おこし協力隊** | 総務省 (特別交付税) → via **御杖村** | ~¥320万/yr 報償費 + ~¥200万/yr activity (~¥520万 total), up to 3 yrs; + up to ¥100万 startup grant | ~3 yrs of funded posts that convert to permanent co-op hires |
| **移住支援金 (Relocation grant)** | 国+県+町 → **the mover** | Household up to ¥100万 + up to ¥100万 per child (<18); single ¥60万 | For movers from Tokyo 23-ku to 御杖 in a registered co-op job |
| **緑の青年就業準備給付金** | 林野庁 → pre-entrants | up to ~¥155万/yr while training | Feeder pipeline from forestry schools |
| **空き家バンク + village housing** | 御杖村 | vacant-house match + renovation support | Solves the housing half of relocation |
| **Project top-up** | project (energy revenue / MoE 交付金) | signing bonus, reno, family support | Differentiator vs. other villages |

### Illustrative stacked package — one relocating family

**地域おこし協力隊 (~¥520万/yr × 3 yrs)** *or* **direct co-op hire + 緑の雇用 training
subsidy** → **plus 移住支援金 ¥100万 + ¥100万/child** → **plus akiya housing + project
top-up.** A genuinely competitive package — and after the 協力隊 term, a permanent
Mitsue Village Forest Association job is waiting (the retention fix most villages lack).

## 4. What has to happen — setup actions (Phase 0–1)

1. **Mitsue Village Forest Association registers as a 緑の雇用 実施事業体** and posts jobs on Nara's matching
   site. *Unlocks both 緑の雇用 and 移住支援金 eligibility.* — Owner: co-op + project.
2. **御杖村 creates 地域おこし協力隊 posts** framed around the forest→energy project
   (forestry + CHP/chip ops + tree-survey/eco-tourism). *A mayor/village decision — ties
   directly into the 官民連携 angle.* — Owner: Representative Director + village.
3. **Confirm 御杖村 / 奈良県 participate in 移住支援金** and are on the matching site.
   — Owner: project + village.
4. **Design the roles as multi-skill** (forestry + energy + field-tech), not single-trade
   — essential for attraction and retention.
5. **Build the mission-story reach**: landing page, media angle, and outreach through the
   project's network and climate-tech / conservation communities.

---

## 5. Honest caveats

- **協力隊 3-year retention is a known nationwide weak spot.** The fix is a real permanent
  job and business model waiting at the end — which is exactly what this project provides.
  Do not create posts without that landing place.
- **移住支援金 has clawback** if the recipient leaves the area within ~5 years — set
  expectations honestly.
- **Roles must be multi-skill.** A single sugi-thinning job will not retain a young
  family; the reforest + energy + tech breadth is what holds people.
- **The mission must be real, not marketing.** These personas detect greenwashing
  instantly — the reforestation must genuinely prioritise native ecology (see
  `project_reforestation_principle`), not just fuel.
