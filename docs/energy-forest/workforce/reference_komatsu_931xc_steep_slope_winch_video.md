## Why it matters
- **Rob's position (2026-10-31):** this applies to any operator, not only Kinjo's daughter. The argument that Mitsue's slopes are too steep for mechanised thinning no longer holds: a Komatsu 931XC (8 wheels) with a winch makes much more possible, even on soft soil.
- Shows the machine class (Komatsu 901/931 harvester) used with winch assist on steep terrain, as on Mitsue's slopes.
- Relevant to operator training ideas (see `docs/outreach/kinjo_note_harvester_operator_idea_2026-10-09.md`) and the workforce plan (`mitsue_forest_workforce_energy_plan.md`).
- Counters "too steep to mechanise" as a reason for the poor thinning volume; supports routing more thinning volume to CHP fuel.
