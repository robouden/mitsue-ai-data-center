# 緑の雇用 (Green Employment) — FY2026 (R8) Notes for Mitsue

National forestry-trainee subsidy run by the Forestry Agency (林野庁), administered by 全国森林組合連合会 (Zenmori). Relevant because Tokuda Rinsan (徳田林産) lists it, and doubling the forest crew ([workforce plan](mitsue_forest_workforce_energy_plan.md)) likely runs through it.

Sources: https://www.ringyou.net/project/ · https://www.ringyou.net/jigyoutai/aramashi/30_page.php (R8 notice, 2026-02-19) · https://www.ringyou.net/jigyoutai/aramashi/annex/30_file1.pdf · https://www.ringyou.net/jigyoutai/aramashi/annex/30_file2.pdf · https://www.ringyou.net/online/images/pamphlet_2025.pdf

## Program
- Running since 2003. Employer-hosted: trainees are employed by an approved 林業経営体 (forestry company or forest coop). Approval is renewed every fiscal year.
- Courses: トライアル雇用, 多能工化, FW (フォレストワーカー, 3 years, OJT + classroom), FL (フォレストリーダー, ~5+ yrs), FM (フォレストマネージャー, ~10+ yrs).
- Consultation: prefectural 林業労働力確保支援センター; list at https://www.ringyou.net/center/list.php

## Employer application (R8)
- Submit (予備)登録申請書 via the regional coordinating body, listing only trainees certain to attend (FW/FL/FM/多能工化).
- Form items, including 森林経営管理制度への対応, are scored; slots may be allocated by score if budget is short. (FY2025: all applicants were accepted.)
- Submit the Forestry Agency work-safety checklist (農林水産業・食品産業の作業安全のための規範, 林業).

## R8 tightening
- **Retention:** counts only trainees still employed by the same company (moving to another forestry firm = left). If 5-year retention <50% and >5 people left, the company's FW1 trainees are rejected.
- **Instructors:** OJT instructors must have completed FL/FM; the "みなし指導員" (in-progress) allowance is removed.
- **Ratio:** 1–2 trainees: 1 instructor; 3–4: 2; 5+: 3.
- **Accidents:** a death or grade-4 disability stops training and withholds the subsidy.
- **Small cohorts:** ≤2 trainees per year in a prefecture attend another prefecture's classroom course, no travel subsidy.
