# Tenkawa Village (天川村) Council Timeline — from 広報てんかわ 議会だより

**Source note (read first):** Tenkawa does not publish full council minutes (議事録) online. Everything below is derived from the "議会だより" (council news) section of 広報てんかわ, the monthly public-relations bulletin — a **summary** written by village staff, typically covering: session dates, agenda items voted on (認定/可決/同意 — approved/passed/consented), budget figures, and occasionally a one-paragraph 一般質問 (general-question) exchange. It is **not a verbatim transcript** and does not consistently name bid amounts, bidder counts, or contractors the way Mitsue's full 議事録 archive does. Treat every claim below as bounded by what this specific bulletin chose to report.

Coverage: issue #320 (2003-09-01) through #593 (2026-07-07), 274 issues, of which 124 contained an identifiable 議会だより section (see README for the other 150).

---

## 2007-11 — Muramoto Construction (村本建設) wins Tenkawa's Central water-supply contract, all 3 zones, via designated bid

**Issue #370 (2007-11-30)**, reporting on the 平成19年第2回天川村議会臨時会 (2nd extraordinary session, FY2007), convened 2007-11-16:

> 平成19年11月12日に中央簡易水道施設整備（１工区）・（2工区）・（3工区）工事の指名競争入札を実施した結果、（１工区）・（2工区）・（3工区）とも村本建設株式会社奈良本店が落札し、請負契約の締結について議会の議決を求めたものです。

Translation: on 2007-11-12 the village ran a **指名競争入札** (designated/invited competitive bid — a narrower process than open bidding, restricted to a pre-selected list of firms) for the Central-district simple water-supply construction project, split into 3 work zones. **村本建設株式会社奈良本店 (Muramoto Construction Co., Nara Main Branch) won all three zones.** The council was asked to approve (and did approve, 可決) the three resulting construction contracts in this same session. Elsewhere in the same issue, a companion news item states the project would run roughly December 2007 through end of March 2010 (3-year pipe-laying project).

This is the only mention of 村本建設 found anywhere in the 274-issue archive (2003–2026) — confirmed by a full-corpus grep, not just the council-section window. No other Tenkawa contract award naming this company was found before or after.

**Relevance to the 2009 bribery case:** this contract (awarded Nov 2007, active through ~March 2010) falls squarely within the window of, and appears to be the same general period as, the Muramoto Construction bribery case that ended in a Nara District Court conviction (suspended sentence, 2009-04-02) of a former Muramoto sales manager on bribery charges connected to a Tenkawa public-works contract. **This bulletin does not name the case, the conviction, or any court proceeding anywhere in the corpus** — confirmed absent via keyword search (贈収賄/収賄/起訴/判決/地裁/有罪 — see README Limitations) across all 2006-2010 issues. Whether the water-supply contract reported in #370 is *the same* contract at issue in the criminal case is **not established by this bulletin alone** — the village PR bulletin, unsurprisingly, does not report on its own contractor's criminal case. This would need to be cross-checked against outside sources (news archives, court records) to confirm the specific project named in the prosecution.

**All-bidder-single-winner pattern**: the phrasing "とも...落札" ("all [zones] were won by...") plus the designated-bid (指名競争入札, not open 一般競争入札) method for all three zones going to the same firm is the same shape of finding Rob flagged as noteworthy in the Mitsue archive (single-bidder-style concentration + narrower bid process). Worth flagging even though the bulletin does not give bid amounts, estimate ratios, or a bidder count — data the Mitsue full-minutes archive had but this summary bulletin does not.

## Recurring generic bid-registration notices (not individual contract news)

Across the corpus, "指名競争入札" appears in 21 places, "一般競争入札" in 3, and "随意契約" in 5 (20 issues total contain at least one). **Almost all of these are the village's periodic "指名競争入札参加資格審査" notices** — a standing invitation for firms to register as qualified bidders for future work (issues #336, #359, #390, #432, #436, #444, #447, #455, #461, #479, #480, #491, #503, #515, #527, #551, #563, #587) — not reports of a specific contract being awarded. **Issue #370 (the Muramoto water-supply award, above) is the only one of these 20 issues that reports an actual awarded contract with a named winning contractor.** This is a structural feature of the bulletin, not a gap in this research pass: 広報てんかわ mostly doesn't publish contract-award detail the way Mitsue's full minutes do.

## General findings on session cadence and content

- Council sessions reported in the bulletin follow the standard Japanese municipal pattern: quarterly 定例会 (regular sessions, typically March/June/September/December) plus occasional 臨時会 (extraordinary sessions) for time-sensitive items like contract approvals (as in #370) or supplementary budgets.
- 124 of 274 issues (45%) carried an identifiable 議会だより section; the other 150 are months with no council-news content in that issue (either no session that month, or the section was folded into village-news pages without the standard heading — see README Limitations).
- Recurring agenda themes across the archive: annual/supplementary budget approvals (予算), settlement-of-accounts approvals for the general and several special accounts (決算の認定 — national health insurance, nursing-care insurance, sewerage, forestry-related special accounts), ordinance amendments (条例の一部改正 — frequently driven by national ministry-ordinance changes cascading down), and consent items for education-board/committee appointments (同意).
- No named individual dispute, no-confidence motion, or contested vote was found in the sampled council-news text; votes are almost always reported as unanimous/uncontested ("原案のとおり可決", "全会一致").
