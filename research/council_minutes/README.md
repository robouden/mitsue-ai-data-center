# Mitsue Village Council Minutes — Research Summary

Source: 100 PDFs from the village council minutes archive (https://www.vill.mitsue.nara.jp/kurashi/annai/gikaijimukyoku/1/1/336.html), covering H29/2017 through R8/2026 (this is a superset of the H31/2019-R8/2026 range originally scoped — 22 extra Heisei-era files from 2017-2018 were also present in the link list and were processed for free).

## Pipeline
1. **Download**: all 100 PDFs pulled with `curl`, politeness delay 0.3s. All 100 succeeded (verified valid PDF, none under 1KB).
2. **Extract**: `pdftotext -layout` on all 100. **All 100 extracted with substantial real text** — no scanned/image-only PDFs were found, so no OCR fallback was needed anywhere in this set.
3. **Year resolution**: every file's true meeting year was parsed from the document's own header (令和/平成 era text), not the filename, because filenames are hand-typed and inconsistent. All 100 resolved successfully (era normalization also had to handle full-width digits and stray internal whitespace like "平成２ ９ 年").
4. Organized into `by_year/2017/` … `by_year/2026/` as symlinks to the `pdf/` and `text/` originals.

## Files in this folder
- `keyword_hits.csv` (11,483 rows) — every line in every transcript containing one of: 予算/補正予算/決算/契約/工事/入札/財産/取得/補助金/基金/繰越, with file, year, line number, matched keyword, and the context line. This is the raw scan everything else was built from.
- `master_spreadsheet.csv` (1,483 rows) — keyword hits that also had a department tag and/or a parsed yen amount, restructured into Date/Meeting/Agenda/Department/Yen_Amount/Type/Speaker/Nakashima_Role/Notes/Source_File/Page.
- `nakashima_log.csv` (58 rows) — every located speaking turn by 中嶋英樹 (Nakashima Hideki), as 総務課長 (General Affairs Chief, through Dec 2022) and as 副村長 (Vice Mayor, from Jan 2023 onward — confirmed via full-name cross-check, not just title). Columns: file, year, page, role, topic (nearest agenda heading), money mentioned, why he spoke (explaining/answering question/objecting/proposing/other), text excerpt.
- `contractors.csv` (12 rows, deduplicated) — every 契約-adjacent mention of a company with a legal suffix (株式会社/有限会社/合同会社), with amount, bid method, department, and project heading. **Amounts in this file are approximate** (see Reliability_Note column and Limitations below) — cross-verify against the source PDF before using a figure from this file specifically.
- `people.csv` (20 rows) — every recurring named speaker (mayor, vice mayor, department chiefs, council chairs/members) with position, years active, and usual topics.
- `projects.csv` (9 rows) — the >Y10M projects that could be traced to a confirmed contract-award figure quoted directly in the transcripts, with contractor, bid method, department, first-seen meeting, and completion status. These figures were manually verified by reading the source paragraph (not just regex-extracted), because the Japanese counting-unit money format (億/万/千円, e.g. "１億２３２１万８千円") made bulk extraction unreliable for exact contract sums — see Limitations.
- `timeline.md` — chronological narrative of the >Y10M project milestones, with cross-cutting observations.

## Nakashima (中嶋英樹) — title transition confirmed
- As **総務課長 (General Affairs Chief)**: earliest occurrence in this set 2020, last occurrence Dec 2022 (`R4-12kaigiroku`).
- As **副村長 (Vice Mayor)**: first occurrence Jan 2023 (`R5-1kaigiroku`), continuing through the most recent 2026 files.
- His predecessor as Vice Mayor, 松原永治, was last seen in the role in 2021 — there is an apparent 2021-2022 gap before 中嶋 took the Vice Mayor post in 2023, which this document set does not explain (worth checking village personnel records directly).
- Across 58 tracked appearances he is almost always the one giving the detailed technical explanation (ご説明申し上げます) immediately after the Mayor gives the brief proposal rationale — the Mayor proposes, Nakashima explains/defends in Q&A. No instance of him objecting to a village position was found; he is consistently on the explaining/defending side, never the challenging side.

## Top-line/notable findings
1. **松塚建設株式会社** (Matsuzuka Construction) is the most-recurring contractor found — it won 3 separate village contracts across 2 different years (2020 school JV partner, 2023 public toilet, 2023 recreation gym seismic retrofit).
2. **村本建設株式会社** (Muramoto Construction) won the 2019 Tsuchiyahara hall (Y82.83M, solo) and the 2020 school integration (Y880M, JV with Matsuzuka). **Correction (2026-07-09)**: the 2024 health center renovation (Y135.5M) was originally mis-attributed to this same JV in an earlier draft — it was actually won by an unrelated contractor, **株式会社中尾組** (Nakao-gumi, Sakurai City); Muramoto/Matsuzuka do not recur a third time. Fixed in `projects.csv`, `timeline.md`, and here.
3. **Single-bidder pattern, verified across all 100 transcripts (2026-07-09 follow-up)**: only 3 contracts in the entire archive drew exactly one bidder — the 2019 Tsuchiyahara hall (99.97% of estimate), the 2020 school (99.74% of estimate, corrected from an earlier draft's erroneous 92.07%), and a 2023 school bus purchase (81.8% of estimate, no debate). The "near-100%-of-estimate + single bidder" combination that drew councilor 木村忠雄's on-record objection both times is isolated to the **2019 and 2020 cases only**, both involving 村本建設 as (co-)winner — every other >Y10M contract checked (Sugano gym, Kamisue gym, health center, water pipe, single-person housing) had 2+ bidders or an openly-narrower designated-bid process, landing at 81-93% of estimate. This is a specific, recurring configuration tied to one contractor and one councilor's scrutiny — not a village-wide bidding problem.
4. **中嶋建設株式会社 surname coincidence**: the 2023 Momomata water-pipe replacement contract (Y50.93M) was awarded to 中嶋建設株式会社 (rep. 中嶋敏治) via a narrower designated/invited-bidder process (指名競争入札), rather than the general open-bid method used for the sibling toilet and gym contracts decided the same council session. The company's surname matches the sitting Vice Mayor 中嶋英樹. A follow-up public-records check (see below) found no confirmed link — treat as an open, unconfirmed coincidence, not a finding.

## Follow-up: 中嶋建設株式会社 lead (public-records check, 2026-07-09)
Checked public sources (gBizINFO corporate registry, business-directory sites, general web search) for any link between 中嶋建設株式会社 (rep. 中嶋敏治) and Vice Mayor 中嶋英樹.
- **Company is real and genuinely local**: registered at 奈良県宇陀郡御杖村大字土屋原４８０番地の１ (Tsuchiyahara, the same small village), corporate number 4150001009955, ~5 employees, licensed for civil/building/road/water-facility construction work. [gBizINFO](https://info.gbiz.go.jp/hojin/ichiran?hojinBango=4150001009955), [Tsukulink profile](https://tsukulink.net/nara/city_293865/100237), [Kensetumap profile](https://www.kensetumap.com/company/397910/profile.php).
- **No public officer/family-relationship data exists** — gBizINFO and the directory sites list only the company name, address, and license categories, not shareholders or family ties. No news article, blog, or registry linking 中嶋敏治 to 中嶋英樹 was found. A web search for the Vice Mayor's own background (中嶋英樹 御杖村 副村長 経歴) turned up nothing — he has essentially no public biography online outside these council minutes.
- **Re-read the actual transcript** (`text/R5-6kaigiroku.txt`, lines ~693-702): this contract was proposed by Mayor 伊藤収宜 and explained in full technical detail by **住民生活課長 仲子雄史** (Residents' Life Division Chief), not by Nakashima. There was no question ("質疑なし"), no debate ("討論なし"), unanimous standing-vote approval. **Nakashima does not appear anywhere in this agenda item's record** — he neither proposed, explained, nor was asked about it. That's the normal pattern for department-level contracts in this archive (department chief explains, not the Vice Mayor), so his absence here isn't itself unusual — but it does mean there is nothing in the minutes tying him personally to this award.
- **Bottom line**: the only concrete link is the surname + shared tiny village (population small enough that shared surnames are common and not inherently meaningful). In a hamlet this size, family relationships between officials and local contractors are plausible but would need a source outside these minutes and outside what's publicly indexed online — e.g. the village's own 戸籍/住民票 records (not public), local reporting, or someone with direct local knowledge (a useful question to just ask a village contact directly, which may be more efficient than further desk research). I'd treat this as closed for further open-web research — diminishing returns — unless Rob has a specific local source to check next.

## Limitations / honesty notes
- **Money-amount extraction is the weakest link.** Japanese public documents write amounts either as plain comma-grouped digits before 円 (e.g. "61,380,000円") or as compound counting units (億/万/千円, e.g. "１億２３２１万８千円" = Y123,218,000). The bulk regex parser in `master_spreadsheet.csv` and `contractors.csv` handles both forms but, in a long speaker turn with multiple numbers, can still pick up the wrong figure in that turn (a estimate/percentage/date number instead of the actual contract sum) or a stray fragment from another sentence. Where this mattered — the projects in `projects.csv` and `timeline.md` — I manually re-read the source paragraph in the PDF text and quote the number directly from the "契約金額、税込み◯◯円で" sentence, which is a reliable fixed phrase used consistently across meetings.
- **Speaker/turn segmentation** is based on the `○役職（氏名）：` marker pattern, which is consistent in this archive, but page-number footers, table-of-contents entries, and column-order artifacts from `pdftotext -layout` occasionally interleave with body text. Where a `heading`/topic field looks like a stale table-of-contents line rather than the live agenda item, treat it as approximate context, not a precise citation.
- **`master_spreadsheet.csv` is large (1,483 rows) and unfiltered by importance** — it is a working dataset, not a curated project list. Use `projects.csv` and `timeline.md` for the vetted >Y10M project narrative; use `master_spreadsheet.csv` / `keyword_hits.csv` only when you need to trace a specific detail back to its source line.
- **Contractor company-name capture is imprecise for JV members**: joint-venture partner names sometimes got captured with a location/representative-title prefix attached (e.g. "代表者北葛城郡広陵町村本建設株式会社" instead of the clean "村本建設株式会社"). I manually normalized the marquee cases in `projects.csv`/`timeline.md`/`README.md`, but `contractors.csv` itself still has the raw, unnormalized capture — check both possible name forms if searching that file.
- **No PDFs required OCR** — all 100 had clean, real text layers; this is a non-issue for this archive.
- **Coverage for 2025-2026 project detail is thin**: only annual budget totals were confidently isolated; no new named >Y10M capital project (beyond the recurring facilities already tracked) was found for this period within the effort budget of this pass. If a specific 2025/2026 project is important to Rob's data-center planning, it should be searched for by name directly in `text/` rather than relying on this pass's automated keyword/money extraction.
- **People.csv years-active are bounded by this archive's date range**, not necessarily true tenure start/end dates (e.g. 伊藤収宜 as Mayor could well predate 2020; the minutes archive simply starts there).
