# Mitsue Village Council — Major Project Timeline (>Y10,000,000)

Chronological milestones for projects exceeding Y10M discussed in the council minutes archive (2017-2026 coverage). Amounts are the contract/award figures actually quoted in the transcripts where located; see `projects.csv` for funding source and contractor detail, and `master_spreadsheet.csv` / `keyword_hits.csv` for the full raw keyword scan this was drawn from.

## 2018 (Heisei 30)
- **Jan 2018** (`3001ka`) — 御杖村定住促進住宅新築工事 (Settlement Promotion Housing) contract awarded to 株式会社坂口組 at **Y72,576,000** via designated competitive bid (指名競争入札).

## 2019 (Reiwa 1 / Heisei 31)
- **Sept 2019** (`R0109kaigiroku`) — 土屋原公民館・体育館耐震改修工事 (Tsuchiyahara Hall/Gym Seismic Retrofit) contract awarded to 村本建設株式会社 at **Y82,830,000**, general competitive bid but only **1 bidder**, landing at 99.97% of the village's own Y82,852,330 estimate. Councilor 木村忠雄 challenged this on the floor as looking like a de facto negotiated contract.

## 2020 (Reiwa 2)
- **Sept 2020** (`0209teireikaigijuroku`) — 御杖村小中学校統合施設整備工事 (School Integration Facility) contract awarded to the 村本・松塚 JV at **Y880,000,000** tax-included, vs a Y882,278,100 tax-included estimate — **99.74% ratio** (corrected; a prior draft of this file mistakenly copied the 2024 health-center ratio here). Again only **1 bidder**. Councilor 木村忠雄 called it one of the ~3 largest-budget projects in village history and raised the same single-bidder concern as in 2019.

## 2022 (Reiwa 4)
- **~June 2022** (`R4-6kaigiroku`) — 菅野体育館・公民館耐震改修工事 (Sugano Gym/Community Hall Seismic Retrofit) contract awarded to 木村組 (Yamatokoriyama) at **Y81,571,600**.
- **Dec 2022** (`R4-12kaigiroku`) — Change order (変更契約) on the Sugano gym contract. This is also the last meeting where 中嶋英樹 appears as **総務課長 (General Affairs Chief)**.

## 2023 (Reiwa 5)
- **Jan 2023** (`R5-1kaigiroku`) — First appearance of 中嶋英樹 as **副村長 (Vice Mayor)**, and budget-line mentions of the Momomata public toilet (Y81.224M planned) and single-person housing project.
- **~May 2023** (`R5-6kaigiroku`) — Same session awards three contracts:
  - 単身者集合住宅新築工事 (Single-Person Housing) → 大和ハウス工業 at **Y134,662,000** (negotiated/プロポーザル方式, not open bid).
  - 桃俣地区公衆トイレ整備工事 (Momomata Public Toilet) → 松塚建設 at **Y75,900,000** (general competitive bid).
  - 桃俣地区配水管布設替工事 (Momomata Water Pipe Replacement) → **中嶋建設株式会社** at **Y50,930,000** (designated/invited competitive bid — narrower pool than the toilet project decided the same day). Surname coincidence with the sitting Vice Mayor — flagged, unconfirmed relationship.
  - 神末レクリェーション体育館耐震改修工事 (Kamisue Rec. Gym Seismic Retrofit) → 松塚建設 at **Y61,380,000** (general competitive bid, 2 bidders).
- **Later 2023** (`R5-4kaigiroku`) — Change orders (変更契約) on both the Momomata toilet and water-pipe contracts (cost adjustments; new totals not isolated in extracted text).
- **Nov 2023** (`R5-11kaigiroku`) — Change order on the Kamisue rec. gym contract.

## 2024 (Reiwa 6)
- **June 2024** (`kaigiroku`) — 御杖村保健福祉医療総合センター長寿命化改修工事 (Health & Welfare Medical Center Renovation) contract awarded to **株式会社中尾組** (Nakao-gumi Co., Sakurai City — CORRECTED; a prior draft of this file mis-attributed this to the 村本・松塚 JV) at **Y135,539,800** tax-included (bid Y123,218,000 pre-tax vs Y133,933,000 estimate, 92.07% ratio — a healthier competitive spread than the 2019/2020 near-100% single-bid cases, and a different contractor entirely).
- **Sept 2023** (`R5-9kaigiroku`) — School bus purchase (スクールバス購入) to 奈良日野自動車株式会社 at **Y17,380,000**, also only **1 bidder**, but at 81.8% of the village's Y21,244,740 estimate (not near-100%) — no debate, no councilor concern raised. Useful control case: a lone bidder alone isn't the red flag; it's the *combination* of one bidder landing within a percent or two of the estimate that drew scrutiny in 2019/2020.
- **Jan 2024** (`R6-1kaigiroku`) — Follow-up mentions of the single-person housing and Momomata toilet projects (progress/settlement carry-over).

## 2025-2026 (Reiwa 7-8)
- Continuing budget-cycle mentions of 桃俣多目的研修センター (Momomata Multipurpose Training Center) usage-fee ordinance changes; no new >Y10M contract award for this facility was located in the extracted text (only fee-schedule housekeeping).
- General account budgets for FY2025 (`kaigirokuR7`) and FY2026 (`2026gikaikaigiroku3gatsu`) approved in the normal March cycle; no new individually-named >Y10M capital project beyond the recurring annual budget totals was isolated for this period within reasoning-effort limits of this pass.

## Cross-cutting observations
1. **村本建設株式会社** (Muramoto Construction) and **松塚建設株式会社** (Matsuzuka Construction) paired as a JV on exactly **one** project in this set — the 2020 school (村本 also won the 2019 hall solo). They did **not** also win the 2024 health center (see correction above) — the "recurring JV" framing in earlier drafts overstated this; it's two appearances, not three.
2. **松塚建設株式会社** alone also won two more mid-size contracts in 2023 (toilet, rec gym) — the single most recurring contractor by file-count in `contractors.csv`.
3. **Single-bidder pattern, checked across all 100 transcripts**: only 3 contracts in the whole archive were found to have drawn exactly one bidder — 2019 Tsuchiyahara hall (99.97% of estimate), 2020 school (99.74% of estimate), and the 2023 school bus purchase (81.8% of estimate, no debate). The near-100%-ratio pattern that drew councilor 木村忠雄's objection is isolated to the **two 2019/2020 cases**, both involving 村本建設 as (co-)winner — it is not a village-wide habit; every other >Y10M contract examined (Sugano gym, Kamisue gym, health center, water pipe, single-person housing) drew 2+ bidders or used a narrower designated-bid method openly, with ratios in the 81-93% range.
4. The 2023 water-pipe contract went to **中嶋建設株式会社**, a company whose name matches the surname of the sitting Vice Mayor 中嶋英樹, via the narrower 指名競争入札 (designated/invited-bidder) method rather than the general open bid used for the sibling projects awarded the same day. Follow-up public-records search (2026-07-09) found the company is a real, independent 5-employee local firm with no public officer/family data linking it to the Vice Mayor, and the Vice Mayor does not appear anywhere in this agenda item's transcript (a department chief proposed and explained it, with no questions asked). Treated as an open, unconfirmed coincidence — see `README.md` for the full write-up.
