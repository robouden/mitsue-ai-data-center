# Research Note — Identifying the Author of the 100-Year Plan Essays (默雷玄明 / 佛國寺)

Tracks the ongoing effort to identify/contact **默雷玄明 (Mokurai Genmyō)**, monk of **佛國寺 (Bukkokuji Temple)**, author of the "100-Year Plan for the Self-Reliant Regeneration of Japanese Farming Villages" (日本農村自立再生百年計画) essay set — see `docs/energy-forest/history/hundred_year_plan_transcription.md` for the full transcribed/translated source text.

---

## What we know for certain (from the source documents)

- Two colophons in the manuscript set, by the same author:
  - "保健計画" (Health Plan) essay, dated Heisei 8 (1996) March 2, signed **默雷 (Mokurai)**, 佛國寺.
  - "林野行政" (Forestry Administration) essay, dated Heisei 9 (1997) February 7, signed **默雷玄明 (Mokurai Genmyō)**, full address **鹿児島県坊津町坊七一三五** (7-1-35 Bō, Bōnotsu-chō, Kagoshima Prefecture).
- The essay "不思議ないのち" (Mysterious Life) in the same set quotes a named authority: **"仏教学者・丘城康四郎翁"**.
- Content/imagery is Zen-flavored: a Daruma ink painting signed 默雷 with the character 喝 (KATSU), Zen koans (cold/heat; a citation that looks like a garbled reference to a Zen classic, possibly 碧巌録 Hekiganroku), "cast into fatal ground to survive" imagery — consistent with **Rinzai** Zen rhetorical style rather than Sōtō.
- Author explicitly self-identifies as "a monk whose great vow is the 100-Year Plan for the Self-Reliant Regeneration of Japanese Farming Villages" (私は「日本農村自立再生百年計画」を大本願とする僧侶).

## What's been checked already, with no result (as of 2026-08-29/30)

- Sōtō-shū national temple directory — no 佛國寺 match in Kagoshima.
- General Kagoshima temple lists, general web search for 佛國寺/默雷玄明 — no match.
- **2026-09-03**: Minamisatsuma City Hall, Bōnotsu branch office (坊津支所市民課地域振興係, staff Miyata) replied to a web inquiry form: **no record** of the temple's current status or any contact for related parties. Dead end via this specific channel.

## New finding — 2026-09-03: the quoted "仏教学者・丘城康四郎翁" is very likely 玉城康四郎 (Tamaki Kōshirō)

The OCR'd name "丘城康四郎" does not match any known scholar, but it is almost certainly a misread of **玉城康四郎 (Tamaki Kōshirō, 1915–1999)** — a real, well-documented figure:
- University of Tokyo professor emeritus, Faculty of Letters / Department of Indian Philosophy (graduated 1940); also taught at Tohoku University and Nihon University.
- Born in **Kumamoto Prefecture** (same Kyushu region as Bōnotsu, Kagoshima).
- His signature scholarly/spiritual theme was specifically **"いのち" (life)** — matching both the title and content of the essay that quotes him ("不思議ないのち" / "Mysterious Life").
- Still in print via publisher **Hōzōkan (法藏館)** — e.g. "悟りと解脱" (Enlightenment and Liberation), with a 2020 reissue whose commentary/afterword (解説) was written by **丘山新 (Okayama/Okuyama Shin)** — a name suspiciously close in kanji to the OCR error ("丘城" vs "丘山"), possibly worth a second look for a coincidence or a real connection, though not yet confirmed as related.

This is the strongest new lead: if Mokurai Genmyō was influenced by or personally connected to Tamaki Kōshirō's circle (a real academic network with living successors, students, and an active publisher), that's a traceable path — unlike the temple itself, which appears to leave no public trace.

## Other unexplored leads, not yet tried

1. **Tamaki Kōshirō connection** (above) — check Hōzōkan's author/imprint pages, and any biographical material (memorial volumes, student associations) for a link to a monk at 佛國寺, Bōnotsu.
2. **Wrong sectarian registry checked previously** — imagery strongly suggests **Rinzai** (or Ōbaku) Zen, not Sōtō. A Rinzai-shū/Myōshin-ji-ha (or Ōbaku-shū) temple registry search has not yet been done.
3. **Address-format discrepancy** — the essay's own colophon gives an old-style banchi address (坊七一三五 / "7135, Bō"), while the city hall's own reply uses a different numbering scheme ("9422番地2"). This could mean pre-address-reorganization numbering. Worth a Kagoshima land-registry (登記) lookup on the historical parcel, the same way `touki.or.jp` (¥331/search) resolved the Mitsue land-ownership question — see [[reference_touki_registry_lookup]].
4. **Bōnotsu Tourist Information Office** (坊津観光案内所, listed on kagoshima-kankou.com) and the **Kishinkan history museum** — flagged as a next step in the original research, never actually contacted.
5. **Kagoshima Prefecture religious-corporation (宗教法人) registry** — if 佛國寺 was ever formally registered and has since dissolved, the prefecture may hold a dissolution record (parallel to how Nara's registry resolved the Mitsue-it NGO name-search question).
6. **National Diet Library / CiNii** — searched for the project's self-given title "日本農村自立再生百年計画" directly; no hits at all. This suggests the manuscript was likely a private/self-published work, never formally distributed or catalogued, so NDL/CiNii searches on the project title are unlikely to help. Not yet tried: searching NDL/CiNii for 默雷玄明 or 佛國寺 as an author name directly (rather than the project title), in case any of his other writing was ever catalogued independently of this manuscript.

## Recommended next single step

Pursue the **Tamaki Kōshirō connection** first — it's the only lead pointing to a documented, living network of people (rather than an apparently-unregistered temple), and could plausibly surface someone who knew or knows of Mokurai Genmyō directly.
