# 議会だより No. 194 (Assembly News, 2026-11-01) — Summary

Source: https://www.vill.mitsue.nara.jp/material/files/group/16/gikaidayori194.pdf (10 pp.; PDF copy and OCR text in `/home/rob/Documents/Mitsue/mitsue_newsletters_cache/gikai_dayori/`). Covers the 3rd regular session of R8 (September 2026). Summary from OCR of vertical text plus reading pp. 1 and 3; pp. 4–10 are OCR-only, so wording and figures there need checking against the PDF.

## Session
- 9 days, convened 2026-09-08, closed 2026-09-16. 16 items handled (petition, ordinance amendments, property lease/acquisition, rules change, supplementary budgets, settlement approvals, report); all 16 resolved during the session.
- 4 members asked general questions (一般質問): Fukuda Maiko, Kageyama Hideaki, Oda Shizuo, Yamaoka Takayoshi.

## Old Mitsue Elementary School (relevant to the project)
- **Property lease approved:** school building and part of the site leased to 青薔薇株式会社 (Ao Bara Co.), selected through last year's public proposal process (p. 3).
- **Petition (陳情):** a villager petitioned the assembly for careful review *before* the lease vote. The むらづくり committee examined 6 points, could not reach a conclusion, and called the Ao Bara representative as a witness on 2026-09-14 about the business's economic viability. The petition was put to a vote with no conclusion reached ("結論を得ず"). The 予算決算委員会 approved the lease on condition that the company strictly follows the basic agreement and lease contract (p. 9).

## Mayor's report (伊藤村長)
- Water: 簡易水道 faces falling revenue and ageing plant; Nara association meeting 7/16 asked for more national support.
- Roads: Route 368 improvement alliance meeting 8/12 (Nabari); requests to MLIT Chubu and Mie in November.
- 8/28: mayor lectured ~130 attendees at Nabari on 御杖村's demand transport (つえみ号).
- FY2025 settlement: all accounts in surplus; warns about future falling local allocation tax.

## General questions
- **Fukuda:** air conditioning in public facilities / heat in shelters. Mayor: 8 of 12 designated shelters have A/C; the 4 without are mostly gymnasiums with low use — uniform installation "difficult at present", though school gym A/C is under study.
- **Kageyama:** Mt. Mitsumine trail upkeep — fallen/insect-killed trees, 五本杉 shelter hut badly decayed; also bears, 防災訓練.
- **Oda:** purchase subsidies (購入助成), river retaining wall repair, bear sightings, joining an association.
- **Yamaoka:** fund management and bond purchases (govt bonds plus time deposits), 15 subsidy programmes, sewage-hauling fee relief.

## Other
- Ordinance: 子ども医療費助成条例 renamed fund reference (社会保険診療報酬支払基金 → 医療情報基盤・診療報酬審査支払機構, effective 2026-10-01).
- 東宇陀環境衛生組合: rule change on cost shares for incinerator upgrade (Uda City, Soni, Mitsue).
- Clinic: electronic endoscope system, ¥8.69M, sole contract with メディセオ (Nara south branch).
- Supplementary budgets: general account +¥27.03M (to ¥2.729bn); 国保 +¥42.9M; 簡易水道 +¥1.4M; 介護 and others.
- Cover story (p. 1): 2026-10-06 study trip by 宇陀郡議会 chairs and all 14 Mitsue/Soni members to Shimokitayama (道の駅 きなりの郷, a 防災道の駅). Bear emergency-hunting: 4 bears seen and 3 caught 9/26–27; their manual was shared as a reference for Mitsue.
