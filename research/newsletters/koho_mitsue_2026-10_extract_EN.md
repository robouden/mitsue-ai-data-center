## 3. 四社神社例大祭 獅子舞奉納 (Shisha Shrine festival lion-dance offering)

- **When:** Sun 25 Oct 2026, 14:00. **Where:** 四社神社, 〒633-1302 宇陀郡御杖村菅野2309 (Sugano gym if rain).
- **Organiser:** 菅野獅子舞保存会. Offered every year on the 4th Sunday of October.
- From early October children and adults practise at night for about 3 weeks; visitors welcome.
- On the day: a long-nosed tengu-like "Hana" (鼻長) character interacts with visitors; the finale is "Oise-mairi" (御伊勢参り), a child dancing standing on an adult's shoulders.
- The day before: 荒神祓 — the lion dance goes round Sugano houses to purify them.
