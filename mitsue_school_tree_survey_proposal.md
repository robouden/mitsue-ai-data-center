# Documenting Mitsue's Trees — A School Program Proposal

**To:** Mitsue Elementary School (and Junior High) — 〒633-1302 奈良県宇陀郡御杖村大字菅野2470 · Tel (0745)-95-2516 · mitsuesyogakkou@vill.mitsue.nara.jp
**From:** Rob Oudendijk — Mitsue Mother Tree Project · **Date:** 2026-07-02

---

## 1. In one sentence

We invite Mitsue's schoolchildren to become the village's **young forest scientists** —
walking their own mountains to find, photograph and map Mitsue's great native trees,
using a simple bilingual phone/tablet app and a printed field guide, as a **summer
project** or a recurring part of the **school year**.

## 2. Why this matters for Mitsue

- **The forest is Mitsue's future.** The village's renewable-energy plan and the Mother
  Tree Project aim to replant native broadleaf forest that feeds wildlife and holds the
  soil and water. To replant well we must first **know which mother trees we already
  have** — and children are the perfect people to find them.
- **The school sits in the forest.** Mitsue Elementary is in Sugano (菅野) — the same
  valley as the project's reforestation demonstration area. The study site is out the window.
- **Real data, real pride.** The children's records become part of the village's official
  tree map and seed-collection plan. Their names stay attached to every tree they log — a
  lasting contribution, not a worksheet.

## 3. What the children actually do

For each tree they find:

1. **Identify it** — with the printed *Heritage Tree Field Guide* (leaf, bark, fruit) —
   12 native species with photos.
2. **Locate it** — one tap on the tablet captures GPS automatically.
3. **Measure it** — trunk width, height, estimated age.
4. **Observe it** — seeds, saplings, hollows, wildlife signs, sacred trees.
5. **Photograph it** — whole tree, bark, leaf, fruit.
6. **Save it** — the record joins the village map.

Everything is bilingual and every field has a plain-language tooltip, so no prior
botany knowledge is needed.

## 4. How it fits the curriculum

| Subject | Link |
|---|---|
| Science (生活科・理科) | Observing living things, seasons, measurement, classification |
| Integrated Studies (総合的な学習の時間) | A term-long inquiry into local community & environment |
| Social / hometown studies | Knowing Mitsue's land, place names, sacred trees |
| Maths | Real measurement, tallying, simple graphs of the data |
| ICT (GIGA tablets) | Purposeful device use: GPS, camera, data entry |
| Language | Bilingual labels; writing up findings |

## 5. Two ways to run it

**A. Summer project (free-study / 自由研究).** Each child, with their family, logs 3–10
trees near home over the holidays, then presents their favourite "mother tree" in September.

**B. Term activity.** 2–3 guided outings per term with the whole class; the map grows year
on year and becomes the school's own long-term dataset.

Either can start small — **one class, one afternoon, five trees.**

## 6. What the Mother Tree Project provides

- The **web app** (https://trees.mitsue.it) — free, bilingual, works in any phone/tablet
  browser; nothing to install.
- **Named class/child accounts** set up in advance.
- Printed **Heritage Tree Field Guide** + paper backup forms.
- An **introductory session** for teachers and a first guided walk.
- A **map & simple report** of the class's trees each term.
- Guidance on **safe, low-impact** fieldwork.

## 7. What the school provides

- Teacher coordination and class time.
- Tablets or phones (the school's GIGA devices are ideal).
- Consent for outdoor activity and use of children's first names.

## 8. Safety & privacy

- Outings stay on **known, safe paths**; adult supervision throughout; bear/insect
  precautions per season.
- Accounts use a **display name only** (e.g. first name or class). No home addresses are collected.
- Tree GPS points can be **coarsened** for any sacred or vulnerable trees before public display.
- All data stays in the **village project**; nothing is sold or shared commercially.

## 9. Suggested next step

A **20-minute meeting** with the head teacher and the science / integrated-studies lead to
pick a class and a date for one trial afternoon this term. We bring the tablets, the guide,
and set everything up.
