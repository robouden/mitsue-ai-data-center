# Yoshiko Zoet-Suzuki

**Co-Representative Director, Transom**

Yoshiko Zoet-Suzuki co-leads Transom, advising clients from SME to Fortune 500, Japanese and multinational. She teaches at Keio Business School. She researches family business succession at the University of St. Gallen.

Drawing on her own family business background, she advises on leadership transitions across Japan. For more than 15 years, her work has included executive coaching, leadership development and organizational change management.

At Mitsue, she brings this perspective to landowners and village stakeholders. She supports community building and long-term continuity for the project.
