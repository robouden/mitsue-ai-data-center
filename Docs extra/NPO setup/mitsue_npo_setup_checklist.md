# NPO Setup — BIOMASS ENERGY & AI (御杖村)

特定非営利活動法人 (tokutei hieiri katsudo hojin) — Nara Prefecture jurisdiction

---

## Phase 1 — Founding preparation *(~1–2 months)*

- [ ] **Decide on NPO activities category**
  Must fall within 1 of 20 approved 特定非営利活動 categories. BIOMASS ENERGY & AI project likely fits: 'まちづくり推進' (community development), '学術・文化・芸術・スポーツ振興', or '環境保全'. Can list multiple.
  > `Article 2 NPO Law`

- [ ] **Recruit founding members (社員)**
  Minimum 10 members (社員) required. These are voting members — different from directors. Collect name, address, DOB for each.
  
  > `10 members min`
  
- [ ] **Appoint board of directors (理事)**
  Minimum 3 理事 (directors). One becomes 代表理事 (representative director). Relatives cannot fill more than 1/3 of positions.
  > `3 directors min` · `No family concentration`

- [ ] **Appoint auditor (監事)**
  At least 1 監事 required. Cannot be a director or employee. Must not be a relative of any director.
  > `1 auditor min`

- [ ] **Decide on NPO name (名称)**
  Must include 特定非営利活動法人 in full in official name. Check for conflicts with existing NPOs in Nara Pref.

- [ ] **Choose office address (主たる事務所)**
  Mitsue Elementary School address can be used if you have consent. Alternatively use a personal address or co-working space. Must be in Japan.
  > `Office consent letter`

---

## Phase 2 — Drafting core documents *(~1–2 months)*

- [ ] **Draft 定款 (teikan / articles of incorporation)**
  The most critical document. Must include: name, purpose, activities, address, membership rules, board structure, fiscal year, dissolution rules. Template available from Nara Pref.
  > `定款 (required)` · `Nara Pref. template`

- [ ] **Draft 設立趣意書 (establishment purpose statement)**
  Free-form narrative explaining why the NPO is being established, background, and vision. This is where the BIOMASS ENERGY & AI project story goes — forest restoration, school repurposing, data center, community revival.
  > `設立趣意書`

- [ ] **Draft 事業計画書 (activity plan) — first year**
  Concrete list of planned activities for the first fiscal year. Must match the 定款 categories. Include: forest restoration activities, school facility use, community events, etc.
  > `事業計画書`

- [ ] **Draft 収支予算書 (income/expense budget) — first year**
  Budget showing expected revenues (membership fees, donations, grants, project income) and expenses. Must balance.
  > `収支予算書`

- [ ] **Prepare 役員名簿 (director/auditor list)**
  List of all 理事 and 監事 with: full name, address, DOB, occupation. Certified copies of residence certificate (住民票) may be required per prefecture.
  > `役員名簿` · `住民票 (each officer)`

- [ ] **Prepare 社員名簿 (member list)**
  List of all 10+ founding members with name and address.
  > `社員名簿`

---

## Phase 3 — Founding assembly (創立総会) *(1 day — formal meeting)*

- [ ] **Hold 創立総会 (founding general assembly)**
  All founding members meet formally. Must vote on and approve: 定款, first-year 事業計画書 and 収支予算書, election of all 理事 and 監事. Minutes must be recorded.
  > `議事録 (minutes)`

- [ ] **Draft 創立総会議事録 (assembly minutes)**
  Formal minutes showing: date, attendees, items discussed, voting results (unanimous approval typically expected), signatures of chairperson and recorder.
  > `創立総会議事録`

- [ ] **Collect 就任承諾書 from each officer**
  Written consent from each 理事 and 監事 accepting their appointment.
  > `就任承諾書 (each)`

---

## Phase 4 — Application to Nara Prefecture *(~4 months review period)*

- [ ] **Prepare application package (認証申請)**
  Submit to Nara Prefecture 県民文化部 (or similar window). Application must include all required documents below.
  > `No application fee`

- [ ] **Submit: 設立認証申請書**
  The cover application form. Nara Pref. has its own format — download from their NPO support page.
  > `設立認証申請書`

- [ ] **Submit: 定款**
  Finalized articles of incorporation (with amendments from 創立総会 if any).
  > `定款`

- [ ] **Submit: 役員名簿 + 就任承諾書 + 住民票**
  Officer list, consent letters, and certified residence certificates for all 理事 and 監事.
  > `役員書類`

- [ ] **Submit: 社員名簿**
  Full list of 10+ founding members.
  > `社員名簿`

- [ ] **Submit: 設立趣意書**
  Narrative statement of purpose.
  > `設立趣意書`

- [ ] **Submit: 事業計画書 + 収支予算書**
  First-year activity plan and budget.
  > `事業計画`

- [ ] **Submit: 創立総会議事録**
  Certified copy of founding assembly minutes.
  > `議事録`

- [ ] **Public縦覧 period (縦覧)**
  Prefecture displays your application publicly for 2 weeks. Citizens may submit opinions. Passive step — just wait.

- [ ] **Receive 認証書 (certification letter)**
  If approved, Nara Pref. issues 認証書 within ~4 months. If rejected, you receive reasons and can re-apply.
  > `認証書`

---

## Phase 5 — Registration & post-setup *(~2–3 weeks after certification)*

- [ ] **Register with Legal Affairs Bureau (法務局)**
  After receiving 認証書, register at Nara District Legal Affairs Bureau (奈良地方法務局) or nearest branch. This makes the NPO a legal entity.
  > `Registration fee ¥10,000–¥15,000` · `法人設立登記申請書`

- [ ] **Submit 設立登記申請書 to 法務局**
  Required: 認証書 (copy), 定款, 役員名簿, 就任承諾書, 代表理事 印鑑 (seal) registration, registry fee.
  > `設立登記申請書`

- [ ] **Obtain 登記事項証明書 (corporate registry)**
  After registration, obtain certified copy of the corporate registry (like a corporate birth certificate). Needed for bank accounts, contracts, grants.
  > `¥600/copy`

- [ ] **Register NPO seal (法人印)**
  Register official 代表者印 (representative seal) at the 法務局. Purchase a formal NPO seal first.
  > `法人印`

- [ ] **Open NPO bank account**
  Bring 登記事項証明書 + 定款 + 法人印 + ID of representative. Japan Post Bank (ゆうちょ) or regional bank. Avoid online-only banks at first — they often can't handle NPO accounts.
  > `登記事項証明書` · `定款`

- [ ] **Register with tax office (税務署)**
  File 法人設立届出書 at the local 税務署 within 2 months of registration. Also notify Nara Pref. tax office (県税事務所) and Mitsue village office.
  > `法人設立届出書`

- [ ] **Submit 事業報告書 annually to Nara Pref.**
  Every year by end of June: activity report, financial statements, list of officers and members. This maintains 認証 status. Non-filing = potential revocation.
  > `Annual obligation`

- [ ] **Consider applying for 認定NPO status (認定・仮認定)**
  After 1 year of operation, can apply for 認定NPO法人 status from NTA — donors get tax deductions, making fundraising much more effective. Especially relevant for BIOMASS ENERGY & AI project's international donor network.
  > `After 1 year ops`
