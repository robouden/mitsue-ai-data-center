# Articles of Incorporation — Mitsue-kun Project General Incorporated Association

---

## Chapter 1 — General Provisions

**Article 1 (Name)** The association is called 一般社団法人御杖くんプロジェクト, in English "Mitsue-kun Project."

**Article 2 (Principal office)** The principal office is at 【address — former Sugano Elementary School / Mitsue Taiken Koryukan, etc.】, Mitsue-mura, Uda-gun, Nara Prefecture. (If the former Sugano school is used, a written consent from the village is required; the Representative Director's address may be used for now.)

**Article 3 (Method of public notice)** Public notices are given by electronic notice on the association's website.

---

## Chapter 2 — Purpose and Business

**Article 4 (Purpose)** Aiming for the realization of a sustainable society, the association's purpose is to contribute to environmental conservation, the development of digital infrastructure, and economic revitalization in local communities in Japan and overseas. To achieve this purpose, it conducts the businesses in the following Article.

**Article 5 (Business)** To achieve the purpose, the association conducts:

1. Forest restoration, conservation, and the promotion of smart forestry.
2. Construction and operation of regionally distributed microgrids using renewable energy.
3. Attraction, installation, and operational support of decarbonized, community-coexistent distributed data centers.
4. Support for regional revitalization and local-industry activation using digital technology (IT, AI, etc.).
5. Regional development in collaboration with domestic and international governments, municipalities, companies, and civil-society organizations.
6. International cooperation and technology transfer in the fields of environment and energy for overseas communities.
7. Research, study, public relations, and policy recommendation on environmental conservation, sustainable development, and digital transformation.
8. Any and all business incidental or related to the preceding items.

---

## Chapter 3 — Members

**Article 6 (Admission)** A person wishing to become a member submits the prescribed application form and requires the approval of a majority of the directors.

**Article 7 (Expenses)** Members pay the admission fee and membership dues separately set by the general meeting of members.

**Article 8 (Voluntary withdrawal)** A member may withdraw at any time by submitting the prescribed notice of withdrawal.

**Article 9 (Expulsion)** A member who violates laws or these Articles, or who damages the association's honor or acts against its purpose, may be expelled by special resolution of the general meeting.

**Article 10 (Loss of membership)** In addition to the preceding two Articles, membership is lost by death or dissolution, by the consent of all members, or by non-payment of dues for 【one year】 or more.

---

## Chapter 4 — General Meeting of Members

**Article 11 (Composition and authority)** The general meeting comprises all members and resolves matters provided by law and these Articles.

**Article 12 (Convocation)** The ordinary general meeting is held within three months after the end of each fiscal year; extraordinary meetings are held as needed. The Representative Director convenes the meeting, with notice given at least 【one week】 before the meeting date.

**Article 13 (Chair)** The Representative Director chairs the general meeting.

**Article 14 (Voting rights)** Each member has one vote.

**Article 15 (Resolutions)** Ordinary resolutions require the attendance of members holding a majority of the voting rights of all members and the affirmative vote of a majority of those present. Special resolutions (expulsion, amendment of these Articles, dissolution, and other important matters set by law) require the affirmative vote of at least half of all members holding at least two-thirds of all voting rights.

**Article 16 (Minutes)** Minutes of the general meeting are prepared as required by law.

---

## Chapter 5 — Officers (Directors)

**Article 17 (Officers)** The association has three or more directors; one of them is the Representative Director. The association has no board of directors and no auditor.

**Article 18 (Election)** Directors are elected by resolution of the general meeting. The Representative Director is chosen from among the directors by mutual vote of the directors.

**Article 19 (Duties and authority)** The Representative Director represents the association and executes its business. The association's business is decided by a majority of the directors, unless these Articles provide otherwise.

**Article 20 (Term)** A director's term runs until the close of the ordinary general meeting for the last fiscal year ending within two years after election. Reappointment is permitted.

**Article 21 (Removal)** A director may be removed by resolution of the general meeting.

**Article 22 (Remuneration)** Directors' remuneration is determined by resolution of the general meeting.

---

## Chapter 6 — Accounts

**Article 23 (Fiscal year)** The fiscal year runs from 1 April to 31 March of the following year.

**Article 24 (Business plan and budget)** The annual business plan and budget are prepared by the Representative Director and approved by the general meeting before the start of each fiscal year.

**Article 25 (Business report and accounts)** The annual business report and financial statements are prepared by the Representative Director after the end of each fiscal year and approved by the ordinary general meeting.

**Article 26 (No distribution of surplus)** The association distributes no surplus. *(non-profit-type requirement)*

---

## Chapter 7 — Amendment and Dissolution

**Article 27 (Amendment)** These Articles may be amended by special resolution of the general meeting.

**Article 28 (Dissolution)** The association dissolves on grounds set by law and by special resolution of the general meeting.

**Article 29 (Residual assets)** Upon liquidation, the residual assets are donated, by resolution of the general meeting, to the national government or a local public body, or to a public-interest incorporated association/foundation or other entity with a similar purpose. No distribution is made to members. *(non-profit-type requirement)*
