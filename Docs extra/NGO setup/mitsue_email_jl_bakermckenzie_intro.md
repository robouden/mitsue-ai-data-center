# Note to JL Bonnier — Baker McKenzie Introduction Request

**Purpose:** Ask JL Bonnier (NBCUniversal) — a former USJ colleague — whether he has a Baker McKenzie contact who could advise on the Mitsue Project's biomass energy / non-profit legal structure.

---

## Draft — English

**To:** Jean-Louis "JL" Bonnier (personal / professional email)
**Subject:** A small favour — Japan legal introduction

---

Hi JL,

I hope you're well. It's been a while since the USJ days — I'm still in Japan, and for the past few years I've been building something quite different here: a rural revitalization project in a small mountain village in Nara called Mitsue, centred on a biomass CHP energy system, forest restoration, and a small AI data center. It has Joi Ito among its advisors, and we're now formalizing the non-profit structure.

I'm looking for legal guidance on the entity formation and the financing structure, and Baker McKenzie came up as the natural fit — their Tokyo team co-heads renewable energy in Japan and has deep biomass project-finance experience.

I know it's a long shot, but do you by any chance have a contact there you'd feel comfortable introducing me to? Even a name would be helpful. If not, no worries at all — just thought it was worth asking given our connection.

More about the project at [mitsue.it](https://mitsue.it) if you're curious.

Thanks, and hope all is well with you.

Rob

---

## Notes

- **Tone:** warm and personal, not formal. You worked alongside JL — match that register.
- **No attachments.** The project URL is enough; let him look if he's curious.
- **The ask is small and specific:** a name or introduction, not a favour from JL himself.
- **If he asks more:** share the English Introduction A4 only.
- **If he has no contact:** that's fine — the cold inquiry form and email to the pro bono team still stand as a backup.
