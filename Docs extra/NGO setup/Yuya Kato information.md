# Yuya Kato

**Impact Officer, SIIF (Social Impact Investment Foundation)**

Yuya Kato is an Impact Officer at SIIF, Japan's leading social impact investment foundation. SIIF promotes outcome-based financing and system-change investment — approaches that target structural root causes rather than symptoms.

His work focuses on how catalytic capital and outcome funds can unlock social and environmental transformation in Japan, including rural revitalization, ecosystem restoration, and community energy systems.

He is a key contact for the Mitsue-kun Project given SIIF's System Change Investment framework, which aligns closely with Mitsue's integrated approach: sugi forest restoration → biomass CHP → village microgrid → carbon-neutral AI data center.

Specific areas to discuss at the Morgan Lewis event (July 2026):

- Outcome-based financing to close the ¥28M–¥53M funding gap
- SIIF network connections to regional impact funds or government outcome-fund pathways
