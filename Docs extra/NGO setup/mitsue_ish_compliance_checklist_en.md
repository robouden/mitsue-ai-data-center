# DIY Compliance & Registration Verification Checklist

**Goal: confirm the Articles comply with Japanese law and the registration forms are correct — without a lawyer, using Japan's free official checkpoints.**

Related drafts: [Articles of Incorporation](mitsue_teikan_draft_en.md) · [Founding assembly docs](mitsue_founding_assembly_docs_en.md)

---

## A. Articles self-check (before notarization)

**A1 — Mandatory clauses (missing any one voids the Articles):**

- [ ] Name (includes "一般社団法人") — Art. 1
- [ ] Purpose — Art. 4
- [ ] Principal office location — Art. 2
- [ ] Founding members' names/addresses — Art. 31
- [ ] Membership admission/withdrawal rules — Arts. 6–10
- [ ] Public-notice method — Art. 3
- [ ] Fiscal year — Art. 23

**A2 — Non-profit-type (tax-exempt) requirements in the Articles:**

- [ ] States that no surplus is distributed — Art. 26
- [ ] States residual assets go to the state / local body / public-interest entity on dissolution — Art. 29
- [ ] States no distribution of residual assets to members — Art. 29
- [ ] No clause granting special benefit to a specific person or entity

**A3 — Officer / related-party rules (non-profit type, no board, no auditor):**

- [ ] 3+ directors elected — Art. 17
- [ ] At least 2 founding members secured (one person cannot form the association; recruit a substitute second founder if Yuka does not join)
- [ ] ⚠️ Each director-plus-relatives group ≤ 1/3 of all directors — with 3 directors, no two may be relatives
- [ ] Representative Director chosen by mutual vote of directors — Art. 18
- [ ] No board of directors and no auditor (consistent with Art. 17)

**A4 — Name & address pre-checks:**

- [ ] Check for an identical name/address corporation at the Legal Affairs Bureau registry service
- [ ] Office location fixed (if using the former Sugano school, obtain the village's written consent)

---

## B. Free official compliance checkpoints (use all three)

**B1 — Notary pre-review (your built-in legal check):**

- [ ] Email the finished draft Articles to the nearest notary office (Nara / Sakurai) for pre-review
- [ ] Fix every point the notary raises (a notary will not certify non-compliant Articles = a compliance guarantee)
- [ ] Choose **electronic notarization** (faster; note: the ¥40,000 revenue stamp never applies to a 一般社団法人 — it is a 株式会社-only tax)
- [ ] Book the certification appointment; prepare the ~¥52,000 fee (~¥50,000 fee + ~¥2,000 copies; bring registered seal and ID)
- [ ] Note: the notary fee is a statutory cost — it cannot be skipped even if you prepare all documents yourselves (notary certification is mandatory)

**B2 — Legal Affairs Bureau pre-filing guidance:**

- [ ] Book the Bureau's free registration-guidance slot (by reservation, ~20 min)
- [ ] Bring the registration application and all attachments for a pre-check
- [ ] Fix any points raised

**B3 — Cross-check with a free formation generator:**

- [ ] In freee or Money Forward company-formation, select "一般社団法人" and fill the form
- [ ] Compare the auto-generated Articles and application against this draft (confirm they match)

**B4 — (Optional, cheap) non-profit-type tax confirmation:**

- [ ] Confirm non-profit-type eligibility at the tax office's corporate-establishment desk or a spot consultation with a tax accountant

---

## C. Registration-form correctness (application for establishment)

**C1 — The application form:**

- [ ] Use the Bureau's official template (with worked example)
- [ ] Name, principal office, and purpose exactly match the Articles
- [ ] Founding directors' / Representative Director's names & addresses match the minutes and consents
- [ ] Public-notice method matches the Articles
- [ ] "Matters to be registered" stated accurately (attachment or online)

**C2 — Attachments:**

- [ ] Notarized Articles
- [ ] Founding assembly minutes (or founding-members' decision)
- [ ] Document evidencing selection of the Representative Director (directors' mutual-vote record)
- [ ] Consent-to-assume-office letters (all founding directors + Representative Director; no auditor)
- [ ] Representative Director's seal certificate (issued within 3 months)
- [ ] Seal notification (corporate representative's seal)
- [ ] Founding member list

**C3 — Fees & deadlines:**

- [ ] Registration tax **¥60,000** (revenue stamps)
- [ ] File at the Nara District Legal Affairs Bureau **within 2 weeks of the founding assembly**
- [ ] Make the corporate seal in advance (outer ring = name, inner ring = 代表理事之印; within a 1–3 cm square; ~18 mm round)
- [ ] Prepare each founder's personal registered seal and seal certificate

---

## D. Post-registration filings

- [ ] Obtain several certified registry extracts (¥600 each: bank, tax office, grants)
- [ ] Open the corporate bank account (Nanto Bank / Japan Post Bank)
- [ ] File the corporate-establishment notification with the tax office (within 2 months)
- [ ] File the same with the Nara prefectural tax office and the village office
- [ ] Confirm the 13-digit corporate number on the National Tax Agency site
- [ ] (Non-profit type) blue-return approval / payroll-office notification, as needed

---

> **Bottom line:** with every box in **A** ticked, **B1 (notary) + B2 (Bureau guidance)** are the two free reviews that effectively guarantee the Articles and forms are correct — the lawyer-free safety net.
