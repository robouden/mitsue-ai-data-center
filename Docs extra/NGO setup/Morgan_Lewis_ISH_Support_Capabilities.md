# Morgan Lewis: Comprehensive ISH Support Capabilities

## Overview

This document provides a comprehensive overview of Morgan Lewis's capabilities for supporting International Social Humanitarian (ISH) projects and nonprofit organizations in Japan. Morgan Lewis emerges as a strong alternative partner to Baker McKenzie, particularly for ISHs focused on social impact, sustainability, and hybrid business models.

## Executive Summary

Morgan Lewis brings **exceptional pro bono culture** (100% participation rate for 5 consecutive years) combined with **specialized social impact expertise** that makes it an ideal partner for ISH projects. The firm's **dedicated Impact Investing practice**, **Tax-Exempt Organizations expertise**, and **Japan-specific capabilities** provide a robust foundation for supporting socially-driven initiatives.
