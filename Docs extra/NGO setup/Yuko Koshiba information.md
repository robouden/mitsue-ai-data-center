# Yuko Koshiba

**Co-CEO, Philanthropy Advisors Japan**

Yuko Koshiba is Co-CEO of Philanthropy Advisors, a firm advising high-net-worth individuals, family offices, and foundations on strategic philanthropy and impact investing in Japan and across Asia.

She works at the intersection of philanthropic capital, public-interest organizations, and social impact — helping clients structure giving strategies that align with Japan's evolving nonprofit and public-benefit framework, including 公益認定 (public-benefit certification) pathways.

Her expertise is directly relevant to the BIOMASS ENERGY & AI project's current stage: establishing a 一般社団法人, closing a ¥28M–¥53M funding gap, and building toward long-term public-interest designation.

She will be attending the Morgan Lewis impact investing event in Tokyo, July 2026.
