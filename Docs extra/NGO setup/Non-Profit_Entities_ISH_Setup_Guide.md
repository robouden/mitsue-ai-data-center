## General ISH (Ippan Shadan Hojin) Setup Information

### Establishment Process:
- **Typical timeframe:** 2-4 weeks
- **Initial members:** 2 or more (can be reduced to 1 after establishment)
- **Notary certification:** Required for Articles of Incorporation

### Two Types of ISH:
1. **Complete non-profit type ISH:**
   - Prohibited from distributing dividends
   - Residual assets distributed to public entities at dissolution
   - 3+ directors without family relationships
   - No past breaches of these requirements

2. **Common benefit type ISH:**
   - Purpose: Pursuing common benefit among members
   - Collects membership fees
   - No profit-earning activities as primary business
   - No special benefit provision to specific individuals/entities in past
   - 3+ directors without family relationships

### Initial Officers Requirements:
- **Normal ISH:** 1 initial director sufficient
- **For both ISH types:** At least 3 directors required
- **For KSH approval:** Board of directors (3+ directors) + 1 auditor needed from establishment

### Key Documents:
- Articles of Incorporation
- Basic establishment documents only

### After Establishment:
- Initial director confirms lawfulness of establishment
- Registration of establishment

### Requirements for KSH Approval:
- ISH must be established first (unlike Approved NPO Hojin)
- No requirement for operation period as ISH (investigates future plans)
- 3-6 months for KSH authorization process
- Requires Public Support Test documents and member/director approvals
