# BIOMASS ENERGY & AI — Lawyer Meeting Questions (English Version)
*Reforesting in Mitsue*

---

### 🏢 Company Structure and Legal Forms

1. **一般社団法人 vs NPO法人：**
   - What are the actual advantages of 一般社団法人?
   - When is the optimal time to convert to NPO法人 in 5 years?
   - What is the procedure for transitioning from 一般社団法人 to NPO法人?

2. **Company Structure Design：**
   - What are the legal risks and benefits of board composition (1 person vs 3 people)?
   - What is the recommended structure for defining each director's responsibility boundaries?
   - How can fair conflict-of-interest policies be developed?

3. **Shareholders and Directors Overlap：**
   - Can shareholders become directors? Is that wise?
   - Does overlapping directors and shareholders affect future NPO法人 transition?

---

### ⚖️ Legal Compliance and Regulations

4. **Post-establishment regulatory requirements：**
   - What documents are not required for 一般社団法人?
   - What tax office filings must be submitted within deadlines?
   - What legal procedures must be conducted annually?

5. **Permits and Licenses：**
   - What licenses are required for project early-stage activities?
   - What licenses are required for forestry business?
   - What licenses are required for EV charging facilities?

6. **Industry-specific legal requirements：**
   - What legal requirements exist for data center business?
   - What legal requirements exist for forestry business?
   - What legal considerations exist for village government partnerships?

---

### 💰 Tax and Financial

7. **Tax-related：**
   - What are the tax benefits of 一般社団法人?
   - Is NPO法人 conversion actually beneficial for tax-deductible donations?
   - How are carbon credits from forestry restoration projects treated for tax purposes?

8. **Accounting and Reporting：**
   - What accounting systems are commonly used?
   - What is the minimum level of financial reporting required by law?
   - When are auditors required?

---

### 🏛️ Governance and Risk Management

9. **Governance Structure：**
   - What is the commonly used by-default board composition?
   - What are the commonly used by-default policies and procedures that need to be documented?
   - What are the general role boundaries between directors and employees?

10. **Risk Management：**
    - What are directors' general legal responsibilities?
    - How should conflicts of interest be handled?
    - How can common litigation risks be mitigated?

---

### 🌍 International and Project-Specific Requirements

11. **International Elements：**
    - Can foreigners (including Rob) become directors?
    - What are the legal issues with funding from overseas donors?
    - What is the legal structure for overseas partners?

12. **BIOMASS ENERGY & AI project-Specific Legal Issues：**
    - How does partnership with Mitsue village government affect responsibility and governance?
    - What legal requirements exist for forestry restoration business beyond general corporate law?
    - What legal requirements exist for data center business?

---

### 📋 Practical Operational Questions

13. **Daily Operations：**
    - What document management systems are commonly used?
    - How should board meeting records be maintained?
    - What are the legal requirements for employees (if any)?

14. **Funding and Contracts：**
    - Are there differences between NPO法人 and 一般社団法人 for grant applications?
    - What is the general legal advice regarding receiving donations from corporations?
    - What are common contract terms?

---

### 🎯 Transition and Future Planning

15. **Future Growth Planning：**
    - What is the approximate cost for converting to a general NPO法人?
    - What is the outline of a 5-year financial report?
    - How do the four pillars (forest restoration, energy, data center, EV charging) function as an NPO?
    - What are the key financial targets to achieve by Year 5?
    - How much is needed by Year 5?  By Year 5, expenditure reaches 60% of revenue margin — how much is needed by Year 5?
    - What is the approximate capital recovery period for mixed funding (grants vs. loans vs. revenue)?

---

### 📞 Practical Advice

16. **Professional Support：**
    - What is the price range for administrative scrivener and lawyers?
    - Should administrative scrivener be hired even if the project is not yet operational?
    - What type of legal counsel is appropriate for this project's size and scope?

---

### 🔄 Follow-up Questions

17. **For Deeper Understanding：**
    - A friend works for a law firm in this field. Should I collaborate with them for more detailed advice?
    - Should I use a startup-focused law firm or a company law specialist?
    - Can you teach me the best practices for starting a business in Japan?
    - What are Japan's startup best practices?

---

## Advice

1. **Organize questions by category** for your lawyer to distribute
2. **Focus on project-specific questions** (forest restoration, EV charging, data center)
3. **Balance time and cost** - some questions require more detailed legal consultation
4. **Create a follow-up question list** for after the initial meeting
