## English Translation (For Your Reference)

*(Purpose)*
*The purpose of this association is to contribute to environmental conservation, the development of digital infrastructure, and economic revitalization in local communities both in Japan and overseas, aiming for the realization of a sustainable society. To achieve this purpose, the association shall conduct the following businesses:*

1. *Businesses related to forest restoration, conservation, and the promotion of smart forestry.*
2. *Construction and operational businesses of regionally distributed microgrids utilizing renewable energy.*
3. *Attraction, installation, and operational support businesses for decarbonized, community-coexistent, distributed data centers.*
4. *Support businesses for regional revitalization and local industrial activation utilizing digital technologies (IT, AI, etc.).*
5. *Regional development businesses in collaboration with domestic and international governments, local municipalities, corporations, and civil society organizations.*
6. *International cooperation, technical transfer, and support businesses in the fields of environment and energy for overseas communities.*
7. *Research, study, public relations, and policy recommendation businesses regarding environmental conservation, sustainable development, and digital transformation.*
8. *Any and all businesses incidental or related to any of the preceding items.*

## Why This Specific Phrasing Works

- **Bullet (3) matches National Subsidies:** The words *"Community-Coexistent"* (地域共生型) and *"Decarbonized"* (脱炭素型) exactly match the terminology used by the **Ministry of the Environment (MOE)** for their data center grant guidelines [1].
- **Bullet (5) unlocks \*Kyodo\*:** Explicitly stating collaboration with *"Local Municipalities"* (地方自治体) gives the town's legal department a green light to sign an agreement with you.
- **Bullet (6) establishes your NGO status:** This gives you the legal mandate to operate overseas, apply for international grants, and run cross-border environmental programs.

## 1. The Core Legal Template: Articles of Incorporation (Teikan)

Every new association must draft a *Teikan* based on templates aligned with the [Act on General Incorporated Associations](https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/4354/en). It is divided into standard legal chapters: [4]

- Chapter 1: General Rules (*Sōtoku*): States the legal name, the physical address of the principal office (e.g., inside Nara Prefecture), and the exact public notice method. [5, 6]
- Chapter 2: Purpose and Business (*Mokuteki to Jigyou*): Specifies the exact activities the association will conduct (crucial for local tax and licensing considerations).
- Chapter 3: Members (*Shain*): Establishes qualifications for entry, membership dues, and procedures for withdrawal or expulsion. [7, 8]
- Chapter 4: General Meeting of Members (*Shain Sōkai*): Outlines the supreme governing body's authorities, resolution thresholds, and voting structures. [6, 7]
- Chapter 5: Officers (*Yakuin*): Defines the numbers, appointment terms, and roles of Directors (*Rishi*) and Auditors (*Kanjaku*). [4, 6]
- Chapter 6: Accounts (*Kaikei*): Establishes the designated fiscal year and rules for handling business plans and financial statements. [6, 9]

## 2. Prefectural Tax Template Structure (Form No. 23)

Within 15 days of successful registration, you must file the local template known as Form No. 23: Report of Corporate Establishment/Office Setting (*Hōjin Setsuritsu-to Shinkokusho*) with the [Nara Prefectural Tax Office](https://www.pref.nara.lg.jp/n023/14387.html). This localized template requires: [2, 10]

- Corporate Attributes: Head office location, corporate number, and specified business year dates. [2, 9]
- Tax Classification Box: A mandatory checkbox identifying whether the association is structured as "Non-profit type" (*Hi-eiri-gata*) or "Non-profit type alternative" (*Hi-eiri-gata igai*). *This determines how Nara Prefecture assesses your corporate tax requirements.* [9]
- Mandatory Attachments: The template dictates you must attach copies of your certified *Teikan*, a certified copy of your commercial registry (*Rireki Jikō Zenbu Shōmeisho*), and a clear physical location map of your office within Nara Prefecture. [2]

## 3. Step-by-Step Filing Workflow

Following the structural templates, the practical workflow to establish the entity consists of:

1. Drafting: Customize the standard *Teikan* template to reflect your local Nara address and chosen business objectives.
2. Notarization: Bring the *Teikan* template to a Nara Notary Public Office (*Kōshō Yakuba*) for official validation.
3. Registration: Submit the finalized legal dossier to the Nara District Legal Affairs Bureau (*Hōmukyoku*) to formalize the corporate birth.
4. Local Notification: Submit the local Tax Form No. 23 to Nara Prefecture. [3, 10, 11, 12]
