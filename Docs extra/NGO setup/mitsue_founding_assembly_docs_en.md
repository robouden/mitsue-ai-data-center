# Doc 4 — Filing Checklist (Legal Affairs Bureau)

**File at the Nara District Legal Affairs Bureau within 2 weeks of the founding assembly. Registration tax ¥60,000 (revenue stamps).**

- [ ] Application for registration of establishment (一般社団法人設立登記申請書)
- [ ] ¥60,000 in revenue stamps (registration tax) on a backing sheet
- [ ] Notarized Articles of Incorporation — [mitsue_teikan_draft_en.md](mitsue_teikan_draft_en.md) (filed in Japanese)
- [ ] Founding assembly minutes — Doc 1
- [ ] Document evidencing selection of the Representative Director (mutual-vote record of the founding directors)
- [ ] Consent-to-assume-office letters for each founding director and the Representative Director — Doc 2 (no auditor)
- [ ] Seal certificate of the Representative Director (issued within 3 months)
- [ ] Seal notification (corporate representative's seal) — make the corporate seal first (¥5,000–¥15,000)
- [ ] Founding member list — Doc 3
- [ ] (If needed) identity certificate / power of attorney

**After registration:**
- [ ] Obtain certified registry extracts (¥600 each) — several, for bank, tax office, grants
- [ ] Open the corporate bank account (Nanto Bank / Japan Post Bank)
- [ ] File the corporate-establishment notification with the tax office, prefectural tax office, and village office (within 2 months; no fee)
- [ ] Confirm the 13-digit corporate number on the National Tax Agency site (assigned automatically)

---

> **Steps that can't be done on paper:** (1) make the corporate seal; (2) obtain each founder's personal-seal certificate from their city office; (3) book the notary office (Nara / Sakurai) for certification of the Articles — electronic notarization is faster.
