## Official Japanese Text for Your *Teikan*

Copy and paste this text exactly into the "Purpose" article of your document:

> **（目的）**
> **当法人は、持続可能な社会の実現を目指し、国内外の地域社会における環境保全、デジタルインフラの整備、及び経済の活性化に寄与することを目的とし、その目的を達成するため、次の事業を行う。**
>
> **(1) 森林の再生、保全及びスマート林業の推進に関する事業**
> **(2) 再生可能エネルギーを活用した地域分散型マイクログリッドの構築及び運営事業**
> **(3) 脱炭素型及び地域共生型の分散型データセンターの誘致、設置及び運営支援事業**
> **(4) デジタル技術（IT・AI等）を活用した地方創生及び地域産業の活性化支援事業**
> **(5) 国内外の政府、地方自治体、企業及び民間団体との協働による地域開発事業**
> **(6) 国際協力、海外の地域社会への環境・エネルギー分野における技術移転及び支援事業**
> **(7) 環境保全、持続可能な開発及びデジタル移行に関する調査、研究、広報及び政策提言事業**
> **(8) 前各号に掲げる事業に附帯又は関連する一切の事業**

## English Translation (For Your Reference)

*(Purpose)*
*The purpose of this association is to contribute to environmental conservation, the development of digital infrastructure, and economic revitalization in local communities both in Japan and overseas, aiming for the realization of a sustainable society. To achieve this purpose, the association shall conduct the following businesses:*

1. *Businesses related to forest restoration, conservation, and the promotion of smart forestry.*
2. *Construction and operational businesses of regionally distributed microgrids utilizing renewable energy.*
3. *Attraction, installation, and operational support businesses for decarbonized, community-coexistent, distributed data centers.*
4. *Support businesses for regional revitalization and local industrial activation utilizing digital technologies (IT, AI, etc.).*
5. *Regional development businesses in collaboration with domestic and international governments, local municipalities, corporations, and civil society organizations.*
6. *International cooperation, technical transfer, and support businesses in the fields of environment and energy for overseas communities.*
7. *Research, study, public relations, and policy recommendation businesses regarding environmental conservation, sustainable development, and digital transformation.*
8. *Any and all businesses incidental or related to any of the preceding items.*

------

## Why This Specific Phrasing Works

- **Bullet (3) matches National Subsidies:** The words *"Community-Coexistent"* (地域共生型) and *"Decarbonized"* (脱炭素型) exactly match the terminology used by the **Ministry of the Environment (MOE)** for their data center grant guidelines [1].
- **Bullet (5) unlocks \*Kyodo\*:** Explicitly stating collaboration with *"Local Municipalities"* (地方自治体) gives the town's legal department a green light to sign an agreement with you.
- **Bullet (6) establishes your NGO status:** This gives you the legal mandate to operate overseas, apply for international grants, and run cross-border environmental programs.
