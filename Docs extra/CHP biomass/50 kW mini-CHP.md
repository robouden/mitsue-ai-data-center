A **50 kW electrical (kWel) biomass wood chip CHP (Combined Heat and Power) unit** typically operates via small-scale wood gasification. This highly efficient process converts raw wood chips into a clean, combustible syngas to drive an internal combustion engine coupled to a 50 kW generator. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 

## Typical Technical Specifications

For a commercial 50 kWel wood chip CHP unit, you can expect the following technical baselines: [6, 7] 

- 
- **Electrical Output:** ~45 to 56 kWel (alternating current, ready for grid feed-in or on-site use).
- **Thermal Output:** ~95 to 130 kWth (available as hot water up to 90°C for district heating, kilns, or drying systems).
- **Total Efficiency:** ~85% to 90% (leveraging both electricity and recaptured thermal energy).
- **Fuel Consumption:** Roughly 40 to 50 kg of wood chips per hour of continuous operation. [7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12] 
-

## Strict Fuel Requirements for Wood Chips

Unlike large-scale biomass boilers, small-scale 50 kW gasification systems are highly sensitive to fuel quality. For reliable operation without tar buildup, the wood chips must meet strict specifications: [13, 14] 

- 
- **Moisture Content:** Crucially low, typically between **8% and 15%** water content. Standard "green" or fresh wood chips (~40-50% moisture) must undergo a pre-drying process before entering the gasifier.
- **Chip Size:** Highly uniform sizing—most commonly **P31S or P45 standards** (roughly 30–50mm).
- **Fines & Ash:** Low dust/fine material content to prevent system clogging and an ash content below 1%. [6, 7, 13, 15, 16]

## Ideal Use Cases & Economics

These systems are ideal for decentralized, baseload power generation where a year-round, matching demand for heat exists. Common deployments include: [14, 15] 

- 
- **Sawmills and Woodworking Shops:** Utilizing their own dry wood residues as free fuel.
- **Agriculture & District Heating:** Powering local grids while using waste heat to dry crops, warm stables, or heat a village district.
- **Hotels & Wellness Centers:** Facilities with massive, constant thermal needs for pools and hot water. [6, 15, 20, 21, 22]
