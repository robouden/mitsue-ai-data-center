# Mitsue-Kanko (Forest Co-op) Meeting — 5 Aug 2026
## Question list (Phase 0–1 only)

Source: `forest_group_discussion_topics.md` (full version)

---

## Guiding principle (state first — not a question)

Purpose of replanting is ecosystem restoration (restore wildlife food supply), not short-term timber profit. More Trees is in charge of species selection (they have the expertise); co-op plants + tends.

---

## Understand what they do today

- What forestry work do they currently do? (thinning, afforestation, processing, sales)
- Do they already have native broadleaf / Miyawaki-style reforestation expertise, or is their replanting experience sugi-only?
  - Experience with **local-provenance seedlings** (seed-collect + nursery in village, replant on same land)?
  - Experience with **assisted natural regeneration** (no planting — tend existing seedlings)?
  - Familiar with / tried **natural species arrangement** or Miyawaki dense planting?
- Co-op capacity: chippers, operators, crew availability?

## Village government status (raised at More Trees' Kishi-san's request)

- **Does the village government already have its own reforestation/forest-use plan in motion that doesn't involve us or More Trees?**
  More Trees' participation is contingent on village-government go-ahead — need to confirm there isn't an existing separate plan.

## Chip production (supply chain)

- Who does the thinning — co-op, private contractors, or hybrid?
- Equipment needed (own vs. rent vs. contract)?
- Can they meet the CHP's chip sizing standard (P31S / P45, ~30–50 mm)?
- Transport route from forest to processing/storage site?

## Drying & storage

- Who dries the green chips?
- Location — co-located with CHP or a satellite forest site?
- Fire safety / permitting requirements for chip storage?

## Role & relationship

- Role: partner, contractor, or hybrid?
- Willing to plant + tend native broadleaf (species selected by More Trees) for wildlife forage?
- Interested in co-running seedling/nursery work as a school or eco-tour programme?
- Profit-sharing or service-fee model?

## Land & contracts

- Can they help inventory landowners for the parcels involved?
- Any village-owned forest land usable without private contracts?

---

## Precedent to cite: Tenkawa × more trees

Tenkawa Village + Dorogawa Property Ward (洞川財産区) signed a formal cooperation agreement with more trees on 2021-06-07 (Sakamoto Ryuichi himself), still active through 2025 — Kii Peninsula's first more trees village agreement. ~6 ha of clear-cut former ski-resort land replanted with native broadleaf (キハダ), 3,000+ trees over 3 years, funded by rotating corporate sponsors (UGG/Deckers Japan, 三井住友カード, 三井住友銀行, 株式会社ウォーターネット via the GFP carbon-offset scheme). Useful as concrete proof that this exact co-op-plants/NGO-funds model already works in a comparable Nara mountain village — worth mentioning if the co-op is skeptical of the arrangement.
